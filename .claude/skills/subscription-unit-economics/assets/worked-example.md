# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic cohort: 100 customers acquired for USD 2,000. First shipment contributes USD 12/customer = 1,200. Second period has 70 paid shipments contributing USD 12 each = 840. Cumulative contribution 2,040 or 20.40 per original customer versus CAC 20; observed cohort payback occurs by period two. Do not divide 2,040 by the 70 remaining shipments or claim each customer individually paid back.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Return 20.40 per original customer and cohort payback by period two, with the common cohort denominator.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If 70 renewals are scheduled but only 50 paid, use the actual paid contribution and show failed/pending renewals separately.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
