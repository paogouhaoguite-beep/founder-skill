# Merchant Image Rights Register

## Inputs and access
Obtain asset candidates or search brief, intended use/channel/territory/duration, subjects, license evidence and required crops. Search or stock connectors are optional; supplied files and license documents suffice. Do not purchase or download remote media unless within the task scope.

## Selection and rights
Define functional criteria first: subject, product accuracy, visual consistency, composition, crop space, resolution and audience context. Compare candidates against these requirements rather than picking solely by aesthetics.

For each candidate, record creator, source URL/asset ID, acquisition date, exact license/version, commercial/editorial restrictions, attribution, derivative limits and model/property release status. Public availability is not commercial permission. AI-generated assets require actual tool terms and checks for misleading product depiction or recognizable protected elements; generation is not blanket clearance.

Separate inspiration-only references from cleared production assets. Inspect crops at target size and ensure edits do not misrepresent what is sold. Stock lifestyle imagery must not imply a real customer endorsement or show a product variant the merchant does not offer.

## Deliver
Return shortlist with fit rationale, selected crop/use specification, rights evidence and blocked uses. Mark unknown rights as unresolved and use an owned/licensed alternative. This register informs review; it is not a legal clearance opinion. Attribution and license obligations belong with the asset wherever required.
