# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic ad says “Travel pouch USD 39 + free shipping.” The destination defaults to a USD 59 bundle; the USD 39 single pouch is selectable, and free shipping requires USD 60. Two mismatches exist. A defensible ad draft is “Travel pouch from USD 39. Free shipping on orders USD 60+,” linked to the single variant. If 500 matched-page visits produced 10 purchases, observed CVR = 2%; 14/500 after a revision is 2.8%, an observed +0.8 percentage points, not proof of causal lift without a suitable design.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the shipping threshold, keep it in the corrected promise; do not hide the condition to improve message match.
2. Given two serving URLs with different default variants, audit both and retain their traffic shares rather than judging only the newest URL.
