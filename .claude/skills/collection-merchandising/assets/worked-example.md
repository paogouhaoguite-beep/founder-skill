# Synthetic assortment
A travel collection has A: pouch, 20 in stock; B: large suitcase, 1 in stock; C: compact organizer, stock unknown. For a carry-on organization mission, rank A on fit and availability; keep B out of the primary mission block; hold C's availability-dependent placement pending stock evidence. Do not assume C has zero stock or promote B solely for high price.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. A product is in stock only in XS, while the card implies every size. Propose variant-aware availability messaging.
2. Historical clicks favor the first row. Describe position bias before using click rate to justify that same ordering.
