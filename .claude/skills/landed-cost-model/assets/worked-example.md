# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic shipment has 100 usable units costing USD 1,000, freight 200, confirmed duty 100, receiving 50, and 120 recoverable import tax. Landed cost = 1,350 or 13.50/unit. Immediate cash outlay is 1,470. If recoverability is unverified, show cost scenarios 13.50 and 14.70; do not assert a tax entitlement.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Reconcile 1,350 cost to 1,470 cash with the 120 tax bridge.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

When 10 units are rejected and supplier credit is pending, disclose the 90-unit usable denominator and credit uncertainty; never divide by 100 without stating that convention.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
