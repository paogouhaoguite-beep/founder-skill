## Specific changes

A co-marketing campaign agreement brief with value exchange, cost split, data boundaries and deduplicated measurement.

- Identify a customer-use occasion where both products naturally belong. Compare audience relevance and overlap rather than using follower count as partner value.
- Choose a concrete format such as a jointly authored guide, bundle, workshop or referral campaign. Define what each party provides and receives.
- Budget product, shipping, creative, staff and media costs separately; clarify who absorbs returns, refunds and stock shortages.
- Draft an asset/approval schedule and a data-flow map. Separate permission to collaborate from permission to transfer subscriber data.
- Create partner-specific links or codes while preserving one order truth. Define shared reporting and avoid adding each partner’s self-reported conversions.
- Deliver a joint brief with named owners, cancellation conditions and a small pilot. Prepare outreach only where requested; the plan does not send pitches or commit partner funds.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
