# Product video storyboard

> By [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=product-video-storyboard&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) · Practical workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. No ShopChief account required.

## Establish the demonstration promise
Read product facts, use instructions and limitations, real footage/photos, target placement, duration, aspect ratio, audience and action. Use available asset inspection; when footage cannot be viewed, mark suitability unverified. Producing a storyboard requires no generator; rendering requires a separate available editing/video tool.

Choose one buyer question the demonstration answers. List every claim that needs visual proof and the asset or real-world action that could provide it. A generated animation can explain structure; it cannot establish a measured performance result. Do not fabricate an owner testimonial or simulate a real person endorsing the product.

## Build the sequence
Arrange opening context → real action → result/detail → limitation or fit information → next step as the task warrants. Each scene records in/out time, duration, framing/motion, product action, exact narration, on-screen words, audio, source asset, claim evidence and transition. Check that scene durations sum to the requested total and spoken copy can reasonably fit; report estimates if not timed.

Preserve physical continuity: product variant, orientation, included components, fill amount and starting condition. A before/after needs the same relevant conditions and actual evidence. Do not use cuts that hide a material step or exaggerate ease. Plan readable captions and sufficient dwell time; reserve placement-specific UI-safe areas after checking the current destination.

## Deliver something an editor can make
Return the timed storyboard, shot/asset manifest, narration/caption script, missing pickups, music/voice rights needs and export brief. Separate supplied footage, planned filming and synthetic concept frames. A requested variation should change the demonstration angle or buyer question rather than just swapping adjectives.

If rendering is authorized and tools exist, preview the actual artifact for factual continuity, captions and duration. Otherwise deliver the production package and say no final video exists. Paid generation, creator contact and public posting require actual user scope.
