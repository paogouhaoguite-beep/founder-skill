# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Order paid revenue after discount is $100, COGS $35, variable fulfillment/shipping $12 and payment cost $3. Contribution before ads = $50. If 10 of 100 qualified email signups later generate an average $50 contribution, illustrative expected signup value = $5. Do not count that $5 and the final $50 as two independent purchase returns. A refunded order needs the specified restatement.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given margin data is missing, keep a revenue basis and label it instead of manufacturing contribution.

2. Given transactions mix USD and EUR, preserve currency rows or use dated agreed conversion before aggregation.
