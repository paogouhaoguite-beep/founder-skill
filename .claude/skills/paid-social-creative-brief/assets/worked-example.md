# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

A synthetic travel-pouch brand has 12 production hours and USD 600. Three concepts need 3, 4 and 6 hours; they cannot all fit (13 hours). Produce the first two for USD 150 + 200 = 350 and keep the third as an unfunded brief. Existing concept A spent USD 240 for 8 orders with USD 38 pre-ad contribution/order: post-ad contribution is 8 × 38 − 240 = USD 64, before production. A USD 150 new shoot would require 150 / 38 = 3.95 additional pre-ad-contribution orders merely to repay production, before additional media. This is a planning scenario, not a sales forecast.

Hook draft for the documented two-compartment pouch: visual separates cables from toiletries; voice says “I stopped unpacking everything to find one cable”; caption says “Two compartments, one pouch.” Use first-person experience only if the actual creator confirms it; otherwise rewrite as a demonstration.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the 12-hour cap, deliver a slate totaling at most 12 hours and identify the deferred concept; do not silently reduce shooting quality.
2. Given an unverified “waterproof” claim and no test evidence, omit that claim from scripts and list the missing substantiation.
