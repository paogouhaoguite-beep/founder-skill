# Synthetic worked example

Input: P1 and P2 compared bag dimensions with owned laptops; P3 selected by dispatch deadline; P4 discussed both depending on the trip. All are synthetic interview records.

Card A — Fit verifier: checks actual dimensions before purchase (P1, P2, and P4 in commuting context); needs a clearly labeled opening size. Card B — Deadline planner: filters by arrival date (P3 and P4 in travel context); needs destination-specific delivery estimates with uncertainty. P4 belongs to both contexts; do not assign exclusive customer segments.

Decision matrix: dimension diagram serves A; truthful arrival estimate serves B. No age, income or population-share claims can be supported. Revalidate after adding a new product type or shipping region.

Boundary scenario 1: A merchant supplies only a fictional “busy mom” description. Label it an assumption and ask which observed behavior would change the design.
Boundary scenario 2: Two clusters differ in age but require the same information and workflow. Merge them for this decision unless evidence supports a relevant constraint difference.
