# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic finance-approved contract price 120 is allocated using standalone prices goods 100/service 50: goods 80, service 40. Finance supplies goods trigger met this period and service 40 recognized evenly over 4 approved service periods, one completed. Recognized 80+10=90, closing deferred 30, total 120=90+30. These policy assumptions are supplied, not universal rules. If opening deferred 20 on another contract plus new 120 less recognition 90 and approved credit 5, closing 45.

## Boundary case 1

Service evidence is absent although the calendar month ended. Leave service recognition pending rather than automatically release 10.

## Boundary case 2

Customer cash 130 includes 10 collected tax outside this schedule. Do not call deferred balance 40 just because 130−90=40; scoped consideration is 120.
