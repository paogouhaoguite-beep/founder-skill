# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic evidence: opening cash 1,000, contributed capital 1,000 and retained earnings 0, no liabilities. During period, cash revenue 500 and cash operating expenses 300; owner distribution 50; no inventory, taxes, other assets, liabilities or noncash items.

Income: revenue 500 − expenses 300 = net income 200. Cash flow: operating +200; financing distribution −50; net +150. Closing balance sheet: cash 1,150 = equity 1,150. Retained-earnings bridge: 0 + 200 − 50 = 150. Contributed capital remains 1,000, so total equity is 1,150. Cash bridge: 1,000 + 150 = 1,150. All three controls tie for this simplified fixture.

Boundary scenario 1: Opening balance sheet is absent. Do not claim verified cash-flow or retained-earnings bridges.
Boundary scenario 2: Equipment arrives through a noncash financing arrangement. Do not classify the full balance-sheet increase as cash capex; request the financing schedule.
