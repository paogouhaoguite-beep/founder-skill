# Financial Statement Linkage Review

## Required evidence
Collect the trial balance, opening balance sheet, chart mapping, accounting basis, period/currency, revenue and inventory policies, distributions, noncash items, debt/equity/capex movements and approved adjustments. Without enough schedules, prepare only the supported statement and a gap register. Use a spreadsheet locally; no accounting connector is necessary.

## Construct and tie
Map accounts to income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow categories without inventing tax rates or recognition treatments. Preserve expense signs and avoid adding depreciation twice when already included in cost categories. Show revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses and net income under the declared basis.

Build closing assets, liabilities and equity from supported balances. Test assets − liabilities − equity = 0; do not plug retained earnings. Reconcile retained earnings using opening balance, net income, distributions and any separately documented adjustments.

For an indirect cash-flow statement, start with net income, add supported noncash charges and reconcile operating working-capital movements: increases in operating AR/inventory generally reduce cash, increases in operating AP generally increase it, subject to noncash/reclassification adjustments. Separate investing and financing cash flows. Changes in balance-sheet accounts alone may include acquisitions, FX or noncash events and must not automatically become cash flows.

Check three links explicitly: same net income across statements; retained-earnings movement; opening cash + total cash change = closing cash. Disclose unresolved gaps and accountant decisions. A balanced set is not an audit opinion.

## Deliver
Return statements, account mapping, movement bridges and a control table with numbers and pass/fail/unknown. No entries are posted or reports distributed without scope.
