## Specific changes

A capacity- and inventory-aware DTC marketing plan with prioritized initiatives, budget/hour allocation and decision checkpoints.

- Anchor the plan in a physical-product outcome and a baseline: contribution, qualified new buyers, repeat purchase or a named commercial constraint.
- Inventory current work, bottlenecks, viable channels and evidence gaps. Do not score software ARR stages as merchant maturity.
- Choose a small coherent set of initiatives with a buyer job, expected mechanism, evidence and an explicit alternative not pursued.
- Budget money and staff hours across acquisition, content, lifecycle and measurement while respecting inventory/fulfillment limits. Separate committed spend from experimental reserves.
- Give each initiative a deliverable, owner, prerequisite, cap and decision date. Use scenario ranges when outcomes are unknown; no guaranteed growth multiples.
- Return an integrated sequence and review routine tied to actual outcomes, keeping reusable lessons and removing unsupported channel folklore.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
