# Merchant Infographic Production

## Inputs
Collect audience question, verified facts/data, units and dates, target dimensions, brand constraints and image rights. A text/layout specification is the fallback without a graphics tool. Do not invent statistics to fill a layout.

## Information design
Choose structure from relationships: sequence for steps, comparison for alternatives, hierarchy for groups, timeline for dates, or quantitative chart for measured values. Do not use proportional icons or areas that imply unsupported quantities. Write a concise narrative and source-linked content inventory before styling.

Choose the main takeaway, reading order and visual hierarchy. Give every diagram/icon a meaning and label; decorations must not obscure conditions or units. Keep text and numeric precision appropriate to the actual evidence. Reserve readable space for source/date and material limitations.

Produce with available design tools only within scope. Inspect at intended display size and verify all facts, labels, alignment, contrast and reading order against the content inventory. Generated text needs letter-by-letter checking; use editable typesetting when exactness matters.

## Deliver
Return infographic or complete layout/content brief, accessible text alternative, source table and QA status. A polished graphic is not proof the underlying data is representative or causal.
