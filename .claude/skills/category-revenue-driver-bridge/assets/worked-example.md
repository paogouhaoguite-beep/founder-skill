# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic T0=1000,C0=.02,U0=1,P0=50 gives 1000 revenue. T1=1200,C1=.025,U1=1,P1=48 gives 1440. Traffic=(200)(.02)(1)(50)=200; conversion=1200(.005)(1)(50)=300; units=0; price/mix=1200(.025)(1)(−2)=−60. Sum**440**, equal 1440−1000. A content edit coincided with this change, but traffic and conversion also coincide with a promotion; explanation is associative pending causal evidence.

## Boundary case 1

Prior category revenue is zero and orders are zero. Do not divide to create ASP or percentage growth; use absolute totals and new-category analysis.

## Boundary case 2

ASP fell because mix shifted to smaller packs with unchanged SKU prices. Do not report a pure price cut without matched-SKU evidence.
