# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic input: A product brief says “holds 1 L and stays cold 24 hours.” Dated specification S1 lists nominal capacity 1 L. Test report T1 describes 12 hours below its defined temperature limit under one lab setup. Blog B1 repeats T1 but says “all day”.

| Proposition | Primary evidence | Verdict | Safe wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominal capacity is 1 L | S1 specification field | Supported within that specification | Nominal capacity: 1 L |
| Cold retention is 24 hours | T1 measured only 12 hours; B1 derives from T1 | Unresolved for 24 hours | Do not publish a 24-hour claim |

T1 and B1 are one evidence origin. The brief may describe the 12-hour test only with its actual conditions and claim-review approval. T1’s shorter observation window does not prove failure at hour 24.

Boundary scenario 1: The cited report is inaccessible. Record the broken chain and ask for the report; do not infer its result from the title.
Boundary scenario 2: Two studies disagree because they test different temperatures. Preserve both conditions rather than averaging incompatible outcomes.
