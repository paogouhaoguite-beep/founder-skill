# Evidence Verified Research Brief

## Frame and gather
Collect the exact question or claim, product/market/date scope, decision stakes, supplied citations and access limits. Browser research is optional for supplied documents but mandatory for assertions about current rules, prices or platform behavior. Without access, identify missing evidence; never invent a citation or imply a current lookup occurred.

## Identity, document coverage and refresh
For a named company or product, resolve domain, legal entity where relevant, jurisdiction and identifiers before merging evidence; same-name entities remain distinct. For PDFs and long documents, preserve page/section anchors and state extraction coverage; incomplete extraction cannot support a whole-document conclusion. When refreshing a prior brief, hold the decision question and comparison window/source coverage explicit, then report newly supported, superseded, removed and contradicted claims with dates. Do not imply continuous monitoring or silently replace the old evidence record.

## Trace evidence
Split compound claims into atomic propositions while preserving the original meaning. Do not silently strengthen “associated with” to “causes”, invent a numeric meaning for “many”, or treat vague “best” claims as testable without a defined comparison.

Record source title, publisher, date, location and the exact supporting passage or data. Follow secondary citations to original data or documents and record broken links. Group repeated reports by their shared origin. Shared interests may create bias but do not automatically make independently collected evidence identical.

Appraise applicability, methods, population, units, time window, conflicts, corrections and missing comparisons. One authoritative primary record may settle a specific product fact; many repeated articles do not create corroboration. Use supported, partly supported, contradicted, or unresolved verdicts with a reason and what would change the verdict. Lack of access or support is not disproof.

Cluster claims by decision-relevant themes, not one summary per document. For disagreements, compare definitions, populations and periods before treating them as a true contradiction. Retain negative evidence and unanswered questions. Legal, health or product-safety conclusions require current authoritative sources and appropriate specialist review.

## Deliver
Return an atomic claim/source-chain/verdict table, theme synthesis, disagreements, evidence gaps and a recommendation bounded by the evidence. Separate observation, inference and proposed action; citations must let a reader reproduce each material claim. No automatic publication or source outreach.
