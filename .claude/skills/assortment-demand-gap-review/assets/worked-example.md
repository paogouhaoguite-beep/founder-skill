# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic search “travel-size” has 40 unique search sessions and 30 zero-result sessions. Store already sells mini SKU M under “miniature”: first hypothesis is synonym/discovery gap, not a new SKU. Cell “unscented travel” has 15 separate customer requests and no current match: propose a supplier feasibility test, no revenue forecast. Launch cohort A opened 100 units, received 50, sold 60 net units under supplied net-unit definition: sell-through 60/150=40%. CohortB at only one week cannot be judged against A at four weeks without age alignment.

## Boundary case 1

Negative inventory adjustments remove 20 units. Do not count those units as sales or silently change the denominator; reconcile a separate stock movement bridge.

## Boundary case 2

Competitor offers ten sizes while own brand has two. Count difference alone is not a demand gap; require shopper need evidence before expanding variants.
