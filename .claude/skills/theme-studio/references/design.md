# Design decisions

Inspect the existing storefront and merchant references. Explain concrete decisions about type scale, content hierarchy, spacing, contrast, image cropping and section order. Adapt to the product and brand rather than injecting a generic marquee, countdown or invented social proof.

Use factual prices, delivery terms and product attributes. Verify important mobile interactions and readability. Prefer existing theme settings/blocks when they support the requested change. A broad redesign and a small CSS adjustment require different scopes; follow the user's actual request.
