## Specific changes

A voluntary employee advocacy blueprint with opt-in roster, disclosure matrix, differentiated share kit and receipt tracker.

- Choose an opt-in program with an explicit exit path. A manager-provided roster is not evidence that individuals volunteered.
- Separate founder, employee and paid partner roles; record the material connection and necessary disclosure for each. Do not treat unpaid posting as absence of a connection.
- Build a share kit around one substantiated product fact, with distinct first-person angles and fill-in sections. Do not invent employees’ personal product experiences.
- Match each image to its permission scope, destination and expiry; draft variations for the channel and advocate rather than identical coordinated posts.
- Let advocates choose their participation and timing. Never plan reciprocal like/comment rings or evaluate staff by personal-account posting quotas.
- Track kit version, volunteer, disclosure, planned window and voluntary posting receipt separately. Count published work only when evidence exists; report clicks and orders separately from participation.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
