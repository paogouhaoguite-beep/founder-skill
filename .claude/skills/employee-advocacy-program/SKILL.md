# Employee Advocacy Program

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a voluntary employee advocacy blueprint with opt-in roster, disclosure matrix, differentiated share kit and receipt tracker.

## Merchant inputs

Brand goal; voluntary participant approvals; owned product facts; permitted images; target channels; each participant’s available time and disclosure wording.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Choose an opt-in program with an explicit exit path. A manager-provided roster is not evidence that individuals volunteered.

2. Separate founder, employee and paid partner roles; record the material connection and necessary disclosure for each. Do not treat unpaid posting as absence of a connection.

3. Build a share kit around one substantiated product fact, with distinct first-person angles and fill-in sections. Do not invent employees’ personal product experiences.

4. Match each image to its permission scope, destination and expiry; draft variations for the channel and advocate rather than identical coordinated posts.

5. Let advocates choose their participation and timing. Never plan reciprocal like/comment rings or evaluate staff by personal-account posting quotas.

6. Track kit version, volunteer, disclosure, planned window and voluntary posting receipt separately. Count published work only when evidence exists; report clicks and orders separately from participation.

## Deliverable

A voluntary employee advocacy blueprint with opt-in roster, disclosure matrix, differentiated share kit and receipt tracker.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
