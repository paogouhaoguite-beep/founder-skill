# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic daily demand is 4 units; lead time 10 days, review interval 5 days, buffer 12. Available 30; confirmed receipt 10 arrives day 5; no unallocated backlog. Target = 4 × 15 + 12 = 72. Raw requirement = 72 - 30 - 10 = 32. A case pack of 12 gives 36 ordered, 4 above the raw requirement. Before the new order arrives on day 10, demand is 40 and stock plus the existing receipt is 40; there is no pre-arrival safety cushion. Propose 36, subject to budget and confirmation of receipt timing.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

With the inputs above, return 36 units and expose the zero cushion before day 10; do not report 32 as orderable in full cases.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If the 10-unit receipt moves to day 12, identify a day-8-to-day-10 shortage risk and propose an explicit expedite/transfer decision; do not let its later arrival hide the shortage.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
