# Product comparison page

> By [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=product-comparison-page&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) · Practical workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. No ShopChief account required.

## Choose a decision the page can answer
Read the compared SKUs/models, market/currency, audience/use case, merchant relationship and source product specifications. Use current public manufacturer pages or merchant-supplied dated documents. Without live browsing label price/availability stale or unverified. Do not infer a competitor property from silence.

Create a fact matrix before drafting: dimension, materials, fit/capacity, care, warranty/returns, accessories included, landed purchase cost where known, and source/date for every cell. Compare the same model revision and unit basis. Separate measured facts, manufacturer claims and reviewer opinions. No selective comparison of one brand's sale price against another's unrelated list price.

## Write for fit, including tradeoffs
Lead with who each product suits, then a compact comparison and deeper explanations of meaningful differences. Recommend the competitor when supplied evidence supports the buyer's need. State unknowns and the merchant's relationship to its own product. Do not fabricate a hands-on test, star rating or testimonial. A feature checklist is insufficient when a difference has no buying implication.

Draft title, introduction, comparison table, scenario recommendations, honest limitations, FAQs supported by evidence and an appropriate merchant CTA. Avoid mass-producing near-identical pages for unresearched combinations. Schema implementation and keyword portfolio planning are separate scopes.

Deliver a publishable draft plus a claim ledger (`claim | model/market | evidence URL/file | observed date | caveat | update trigger`). Mark volatile price, stock and policy cells for refresh. Publishing, affiliate enrollment and collecting testimonials require actual authorization; unknown trademark or quotation rights are not resolved by this skill.
