# Brand Narrative Change Audit

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a brand narrative transition audit with passage-level drift findings, sequenced asset replacement and post-change evidence checks.

## Merchant inputs

Old and new approved positioning; current pages/emails/ads/packaging; owners; proof sources; rollout dates; observed customer responses.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Classify the change: wording refinement, new differentiation, or a new audience/category. Match the required asset review to its actual scope.

2. Inventory live and planned touchpoints with exact text, URL or file version, audience, owner and date. Separate stale product facts from harmless stylistic variation.

3. Compare promise, terminology, proof and target buyer to the approved message. Record contradictions at passage level; do not assign a universal brand score.

4. Prioritize high-traffic, paid, checkout-adjacent and durable printed assets by exposure, factual risk and replacement cost. Identify paid or printed material that cannot be changed instantly.

5. Create a dependency-ordered rollout with replacement copy, approvals and verification evidence. Preserve old-to-new URLs if domains or paths change and send technical migration work to the site owner.

6. Monitor comprehension and objections against comparable prior samples. Distinguish improved consistency from proven conversion lift; retain conflicting customer reactions and dates.

## Deliverable

A brand narrative transition audit with passage-level drift findings, sequenced asset replacement and post-change evidence checks.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
