## Specific changes

A brand narrative transition audit with passage-level drift findings, sequenced asset replacement and post-change evidence checks.

- Classify the change: wording refinement, new differentiation, or a new audience/category. Match the required asset review to its actual scope.
- Inventory live and planned touchpoints with exact text, URL or file version, audience, owner and date. Separate stale product facts from harmless stylistic variation.
- Compare promise, terminology, proof and target buyer to the approved message. Record contradictions at passage level; do not assign a universal brand score.
- Prioritize high-traffic, paid, checkout-adjacent and durable printed assets by exposure, factual risk and replacement cost. Identify paid or printed material that cannot be changed instantly.
- Create a dependency-ordered rollout with replacement copy, approvals and verification evidence. Preserve old-to-new URLs if domains or paths change and send technical migration work to the site owner.
- Monitor comprehension and objections against comparable prior samples. Distinguish improved consistency from proven conversion lift; retain conflicting customer reactions and dates.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
