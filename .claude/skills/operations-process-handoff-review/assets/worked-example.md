# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic return case R1: intake work 10 min, approval wait 120, inspection work 20, rework 5, close work 5; elapsed 160 min with active value-adding work 35, rework 5 and wait 120. Wait share=75%; value-add share=35/160=21.875%, **not a universal health verdict**. R2 elapsed 40 min. Using midpoint median for two cases, end-to-end median 100 min. An approval stage P90 plus inspection P90 cannot establish process P90. Proposal: owner Support tests an additional approval review window; measure completed-case elapsed and open-case aging, not just inspection speed.

## Boundary case 1

Approval and inspection run in parallel for 20 minutes. Use their union/critical path; do not count 40 elapsed minutes.

## Boundary case 2
