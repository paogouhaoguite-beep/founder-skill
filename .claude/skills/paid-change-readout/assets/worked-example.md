# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Before $1,000 spend/40 purchases = $25 CPA; after $1,200/60 = $20 CPA, a 20% CPA decrease. But a 15% storewide sale began with the change and there is no control. Report better observed CPA, plus a price/promotion confounder; do not credit bidding alone or claim profit improved without margin/refund data.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the after window has only two days of a seven-day conversion lag, defer the mature efficiency conclusion.

2. Given a holdout also improves by the same amount, do not attribute the whole before/after gain to the intervention.
