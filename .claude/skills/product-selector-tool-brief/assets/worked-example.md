# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Paint covers 10 m²/litre/coat. Room wall area is 36 m², 2 coats, 10% merchant-selected allowance. Required = 36×2/10×1.10 = 7.92 litres; available cans 2.5 and 5 litres. Suggest 5+2.5+2.5 = 10 litres or two 5-litre cans based on supplied prices; 7.5 litres is insufficient. State that surface condition can change coverage.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given coverage is unknown for textured walls, ask for the relevant rate rather than applying the smooth-wall assumption silently.

2. Given no product meets a measured compatibility requirement, return no match, not the closest incompatible item.
