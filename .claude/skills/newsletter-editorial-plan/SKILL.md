# Newsletter Editorial Plan

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a sustainable newsletter and owned-content editorial plan with source policy, completed sample issue and outcome definitions.

## Merchant inputs

Audience promise; opted-in list; brand expertise; source material; available production hours; podcast/owned content if relevant; destination and commercial objective.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Define a recurring reader value proposition independent of discounts, such as category know-how, care, seasonal use or a useful curated digest.

2. Choose original, curated or hybrid issues based on source access and production capacity. If a podcast supplies material, plan episode/topic/guest evidence and convert only content the merchant can reuse.

3. Build recurring sections with a clear role and one relevant commercial handoff. Summarize external sources with attribution rather than reproducing their articles.

4. Plan a sustainable calendar, editorial owner and fact/rights review. Guest proposals and episode promotion remain drafts until their respective actions are authorized.

5. Draft an actual issue with subject, preheader, concise useful content and CTA; preserve the promise made at signup and relevant preference limits.

6. Measure delivery, clicks to useful content and store outcomes separately. Podcast downloads, newsletter opens and orders have different denominators and cannot be summed as reach.

7. Write subject and preheader as a pair, with the preview extending the promised content. Include substantive commentary on curated links, compare unsubscribe/complaint and meaningful clicks across comparable sends, and avoid inventing default open rates or using rigid mobile character budgets as universal limits.

## Deliverable

A sustainable newsletter and owned-content editorial plan with source policy, completed sample issue and outcome definitions.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
