## Specific changes

A sustainable newsletter and owned-content editorial plan with source policy, completed sample issue and outcome definitions.

- Define a recurring reader value proposition independent of discounts, such as category know-how, care, seasonal use or a useful curated digest.
- Choose original, curated or hybrid issues based on source access and production capacity. If a podcast supplies material, plan episode/topic/guest evidence and convert only content the merchant can reuse.
- Build recurring sections with a clear role and one relevant commercial handoff. Summarize external sources with attribution rather than reproducing their articles.
- Plan a sustainable calendar, editorial owner and fact/rights review. Guest proposals and episode promotion remain drafts until their respective actions are authorized.
- Draft an actual issue with subject, preheader, concise useful content and CTA; preserve the promise made at signup and relevant preference limits.
- Measure delivery, clicks to useful content and store outcomes separately. Podcast downloads, newsletter opens and orders have different denominators and cannot be summed as reach.
- Write subject and preheader as a pair, with the preview extending the promised content. Include substantive commentary on curated links, compare unsubscribe/complaint and meaningful clicks across comparable sends, and avoid inventing default open rates or using rigid mobile character budgets as universal limits.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
