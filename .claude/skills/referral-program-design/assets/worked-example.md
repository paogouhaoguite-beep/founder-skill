# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic USD 80 basket with USD 10 friend discount nets USD 70; variable costs USD 42 leave USD 28. USD 8 referrer credit at assumed 50% redemption costs USD 4 in this simplified discount-value scenario, plus USD 2 program fee: residual contribution USD 22. Full redemption scenario leaves USD 18. The USD 10 friend discount must not be deducted twice. In a pilot, 100 participating referrers send 200 invitations and produce 20 qualified new customers: 2 invitations/referrer × 10% conversion = 0.2 measured customers per participant, not proof of a self-sustaining viral loop.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given a returned referred order before validation, withhold/reverse the pending reward under published terms instead of paying on signup.
2. Given one friend uses two links, assign one qualifying order using declared precedence and do not issue duplicate acquisition rewards.
