# Public Review Response Drafting

## Inputs
Collect the review text, platform, date, product context, merchant policies, voice and any verified internal facts. Review exports are enough; connectors are optional. A rating or silent purchase is not sentiment evidence beyond what it actually records.

## Draft with two audiences
Identify the reviewer’s concrete concern and what future shoppers need to understand. Thank positive reviewers without inventing experiences. For criticism, acknowledge the described issue without conceding unverified facts, arguing or accusing the reviewer of fraud.

Keep public replies free of order numbers, addresses, medical details, payment information and account history. Use a private verified contact route for investigation. A public response may say a concern will be reviewed only when the merchant authorizes that commitment. Never claim a refund, replacement or outreach already happened unless confirmed.

Draft a separate private message listing only the minimum information needed to match the case. If a review may violate platform rules, create an evidence-based moderation request for review against current rules; do not assume disagreement warrants removal. Do not condition help on changing a rating or solicit only positive feedback.

## Deliver
Return review ID, issue, public draft, private follow-up draft, fact checks and action status. A response cadence follows actual merchant capacity rather than a fixed universal deadline. Posting, contacting, reporting and issuing remedies are distinct external actions; drafts alone authorize none.
