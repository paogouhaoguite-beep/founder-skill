# Synthetic worked example

Input: Synthetic one-star review: “The cap arrived cracked and no one answered.” Internal ticket exists but no response or remedy is verified.

Public draft: “I’m sorry to hear that the cap arrived damaged and that you have not received a reply. Please contact our support team through the contact page so we can match the issue to your order and review the next step.”

Private draft: “Please share your order number through this private support channel and, if available, a photo of the cap. We’ll check the applicable remedy once we match the order.” No personal details from the internal ticket appear publicly. No replacement is claimed.

Boundary scenario 1: Merchant thinks the reviewer is a competitor. Keep the reply neutral; a platform report needs evidence and current policy criteria.
Boundary scenario 2: A five-star review mentions a medical benefit. Thank the shopper for feedback without repeating or endorsing the unverified health claim.
