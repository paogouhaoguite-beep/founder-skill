# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Synthetic product: a washable cotton tote with an internal bottle pocket, not leakproof. Stimulusv1: “Keep your bottle upright.” Target: shoppers who carry a bottle to work. Exploratory10-person purposive panel; merchant rule: revise if any respondent concludes the bag is leakproof, and if fewer than7/10 correctly identify upright storage.
Prompts: show stimulus for5seconds then hide it; ask “What benefit do you remember?” then “What would you expect it to do?” Rubric: upright storage correct; prevents leaks incorrect safety/expectation inference. Hypothetical returned counts8/10upright,2/10leakproof imply revise despite80% recall. This illustrates the rule only; no panel was run.
