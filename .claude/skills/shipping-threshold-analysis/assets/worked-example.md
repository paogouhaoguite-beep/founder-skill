# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic baseline basket USD 45 pays USD 5 shipping. Threshold 60 encourages a USD 15 addition with 40% incremental margin, giving USD 6. Forgone shipping revenue is 5, added fulfillment is 1: incremental contribution = 6 - 5 - 1 = 0. AOV rises but contribution does not. This assumes the customer actually adds the item and the baseline carrier cost is unchanged.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Return zero incremental contribution and explicitly reject the claim that USD 15 more revenue means USD 15 more profit.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If there is no basket distribution, supply a sensitivity model and request exports rather than predicting uptake or installing a threshold.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
