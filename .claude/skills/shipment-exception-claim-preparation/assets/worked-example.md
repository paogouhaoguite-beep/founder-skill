# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic order O1 has parcels P1/P2. P1 POD confirms 2 items received; P2 was accepted October 1, last scan October 2, promised October 4, and has no later scan as of October 6. Output: **P2 delayed/whereabouts unconfirmed; O1 partially delivered**, not entire order lost. A replacement item costs 18 plus freight 7 =25 incremental cash; a potential carrier recovery 18 remains contingent.

Customer draft: “Two items in P1 were recorded delivered on October 4. P2 has not received a new carrier scan since October 2. We have prepared a carrier trace request; we will update you at the approved next checkpoint once its status is confirmed.” Claim packet: O1/P2, acceptance/last-scan records, promised date, invoice 18 cost basis if contract permits, missing POD, owner Logistics. No submission performed.

## Boundary case 1

Carrier reports delivered but no POD links to P2. Preserve the conflicting status; request parcel-specific evidence before rejecting the customer report.

## Boundary case 2

Original P2 arrives after replacement was authorized but before dispatch. Reconcile both records and request the merchant disposition; do not dispatch again automatically.
