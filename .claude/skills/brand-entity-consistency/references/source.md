## Specific changes

A brand/entity consistency map with owned-identity evidence, domain roles and precise correction proposals.

- Build an entity inventory separating legal company, trading brand, store, product family and SKU. Similar names do not make them one entity.
- Map authoritative owned identifiers and profiles with evidence of control. Use sameAs only for equivalent identities, not every partner or distributor.
- Audit name, URL, logo and description consistency across controlled surfaces while preserving legitimate locale or sub-brand differences.
- Choose domain roles by audience, market and brand separation. Avoid duplicate sites created merely to occupy results; map content ownership and canonical destination for repeated material.
- Prepare a correction matrix for profiles, structured data and site copy with exact old/new values and owners. Do not invent identifiers or a knowledge-panel entitlement.
- Verify changes on published surfaces after authorized edits; consistent entity data supports clarity but does not guarantee a search knowledge panel or AI mention.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
