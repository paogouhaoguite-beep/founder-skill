## Specific changes

A social inbox triage queue with reply drafts, rule-based moderation proposals, escalation owners and UGC permission gaps.

- Scope channels, export window and response commitments. Identify gaps where closed-platform messages were not supplied.
- Read conversational register before assigning sentiment; sarcasm, jokes and purchase excitement can contain apparently negative words.
- Classify each item by needed action: answer, order support, moderation review, rights inquiry or incident escalation. Base urgency on impact and stated response commitments.
- Draft replies with a concrete helpful next step. Avoid arguing with bait; document no-reply decisions and route safety or legal concerns to the responsible person.
- For moderation proposals cite the applicable house rule and preserve evidence. For UGC reuse record explicit owner permission, exact organic/paid placements, territory and expiry.
- Track proposed, approved, posted and verified states separately. Record each completed reply or moderation action from a receipt; a drafted queue is not cleared work.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
