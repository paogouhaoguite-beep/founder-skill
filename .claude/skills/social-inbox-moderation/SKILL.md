# Social Inbox Moderation

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a social inbox triage queue with reply drafts, rule-based moderation proposals, escalation owners and UGC permission gaps.

## Merchant inputs

Dated comments/DM exports; community rules; approved tone; service ownership; response promises; UGC permissions and intended reuse.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Scope channels, export window and response commitments. Identify gaps where closed-platform messages were not supplied.

2. Read conversational register before assigning sentiment; sarcasm, jokes and purchase excitement can contain apparently negative words.

3. Classify each item by needed action: answer, order support, moderation review, rights inquiry or incident escalation. Base urgency on impact and stated response commitments.

4. Draft replies with a concrete helpful next step. Avoid arguing with bait; document no-reply decisions and route safety or legal concerns to the responsible person.

5. For moderation proposals cite the applicable house rule and preserve evidence. For UGC reuse record explicit owner permission, exact organic/paid placements, territory and expiry.

6. Track proposed, approved, posted and verified states separately. Record each completed reply or moderation action from a receipt; a drafted queue is not cleared work.

## Deliverable

A social inbox triage queue with reply drafts, rule-based moderation proposals, escalation owners and UGC permission gaps.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
