# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A and B each deliver 2,000 emails. A has 80 unique valid clicks; B has 90. Rates are 4.0% and 4.5%; difference 0.5 percentage points, relative +12.5%. Expected report does not declare a reliable winner solely from these counts; use the predeclared interval/test and sample plan. For a refill email, pair “Your refill guide” with “Choose the size that fits your jar”, not a false expiry claim.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given B is sent on a sale day and A a week earlier, label the comparison confounded rather than randomized evidence.

2. Given only privacy-inflated opens differ but clicks/orders do not, avoid claiming commercial lift.
