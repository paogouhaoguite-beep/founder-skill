# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic equal-length, mature click cohorts; all purchases below are click-attributed and view-through credits are excluded: before, 100,000 impressions, USD 1,000, 2,000 clicks, 40 purchases; after, 100,000 impressions, USD 1,000, 2,000 clicks, 20 purchases. CPM remains USD 10, CTR 2%, CPC USD 0.50. Click-to-purchase CVR falls from 2% to 1%; CPA doubles from USD 25 to 50. Even if frequency rises, unchanged click response and a documented out-of-stock best-selling variant make post-click availability a competing explanation. Replacing the hook alone would not resolve that verified inventory fault.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given unchanged CTR/CPC and lower CVR with stockouts, surface inventory/post-click diagnosis before a confident fatigue verdict.
2. Given reach missing, do not fabricate frequency from impressions or use a universal exposure threshold.
