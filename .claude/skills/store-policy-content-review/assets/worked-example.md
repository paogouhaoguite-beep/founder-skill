# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic PDP says “Free returns within 30 days”; approved returns policy says “Requests within 14 days of delivery; customer pays return shipping unless the item is faulty.” Output conflict on both timing and cost. Draft PDP replacement: “Request a return within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item is faulty; see the returns policy for eligibility.” This is copied from supplied policy facts, pending merchant publication. An image-only policy gets a text-equivalent draft, not a newly invented 30-day promise.

## Boundary case 1

A policy URL times out. Mark inaccessible/unverified; do not say policy missing.

## Boundary case 2

Merchant supplies two conflicting approved versions with no effective date. Hold substantive rewrite and ask the policy owner to resolve version authority.
