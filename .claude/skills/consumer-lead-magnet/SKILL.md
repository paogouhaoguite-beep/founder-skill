# Consumer Lead Magnet

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a shopper lead-magnet brief and sample content with transparent capture, delivery and quality measurement.

## Merchant inputs

Buyer question; product category; existing useful content; expertise/evidence; capture goal; email consent wording; budget and fulfilment ability.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Select a narrow shopper problem such as fit, care or material choice that can be solved by the resource itself. Do not gate essential purchase terms or basic safety information.

2. Choose a guide, checklist, comparison or quiz output that matches the buying stage and the merchant’s evidence; avoid a generic ebook created only to collect email.

3. Outline the useful artifact and create a representative completed section before designing its signup page.

4. Explain what the subscriber receives immediately and what later marketing they may opt into; separate access fulfilment from broader consent where required.

5. Design the capture and delivery path, including duplicate requests, missing delivery and unsubscribe behavior. Minimize fields to those actually needed.

6. Measure qualified opt-ins, fulfilment success and downstream outcomes against the cost. List size alone is not value, and downloads are not purchases.

## Deliverable

A shopper lead-magnet brief and sample content with transparent capture, delivery and quality measurement.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
