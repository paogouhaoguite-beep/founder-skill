## Specific changes

A shopper lead-magnet brief and sample content with transparent capture, delivery and quality measurement.

- Select a narrow shopper problem such as fit, care or material choice that can be solved by the resource itself. Do not gate essential purchase terms or basic safety information.
- Choose a guide, checklist, comparison or quiz output that matches the buying stage and the merchant’s evidence; avoid a generic ebook created only to collect email.
- Outline the useful artifact and create a representative completed section before designing its signup page.
- Explain what the subscriber receives immediately and what later marketing they may opt into; separate access fulfilment from broader consent where required.
- Design the capture and delivery path, including duplicate requests, missing delivery and unsubscribe behavior. Minimize fields to those actually needed.
- Measure qualified opt-ins, fulfilment success and downstream outcomes against the cost. List size alone is not value, and downloads are not purchases.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
