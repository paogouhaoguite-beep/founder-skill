# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic DTC sells unit 30 with contribution 12; wholesale unit 20 has contribution 6. A scenario of 100 units moving fromDTC towholesale reduces contribution 600, not revenue loss 1000 plus margin loss 600=1600. Partner proposes allowance 1 on 500 units: cost 500; merchant proposes it only in exchange for documented 500-unit commitment and agreed placement, pending acceptance. Of 10 eligible POs,8 on time,9 in full,7 both: jointOTIF 70%, not 72%. A deduction must reference the contract’s actual assessment method.

## Boundary case 1

Partner price is lower because its pack contains fewer units. Normalize pack and delivery terms before calling it undercutting.

## Boundary case 2

Deduction programme changed midperiod. Apply effective clauses to each shipment; do not use current terms retroactively without contractual evidence.
