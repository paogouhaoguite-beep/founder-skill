# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Budget $2,000; 2 videos at $500 each; samples $160; shipping $40; license $300; paid test $400. Total = $1,900, leaving $100 unallocated. Scenario: 40 incremental orders × $25 contribution before campaign cost = $1,000; contribution after $1,900 campaign cost = −$900. Do not call $3,200 gross revenue profit or add an estimated $800 media value to it.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given a quote omits paid usage, mark licensing unresolved and avoid treating it as a fully comparable cheaper offer.

2. Given expected orders are unsupported, deliver break-even orders: $1,900/$25 = 76, explicitly as a scenario, not a forecast.
