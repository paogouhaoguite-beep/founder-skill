## Specific changes

An all-in creator campaign budget with comparable quote normalization, deliverable plan and explicit contribution scenarios.

- Build an all-in cost ledger separating creator fees, samples at economic cost, shipping, production, licensing, amplification and tools.
- Compare quotes only when market, platform, deliverable count, production effort and rights are compatible. Missing quotes become requests, not invented tier rate cards.
- Allocate the stated cap across eligible deliverables and operational costs. A contingency is explicit and merchant-chosen; all line items reconcile to the total.
- Model revenue and contribution scenarios from supplied clicks, conversion, AOV, returns and variable costs. Keep media-equivalent value separate from cash revenue.
- Tie each deliverable to the campaign objective, acceptance condition and rights window. Reduce scope or test size when the feasible plan exceeds budget.
- Return alternative allocations with tradeoffs and a reallocation rule based on comparable evidence, not follower tier or an automatic “winner” label.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
