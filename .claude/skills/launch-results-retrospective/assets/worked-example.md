# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Launch target 50 orders; order truth has 42 paid orders, of which two distinct orders were fully refunded, so retained orders = 40. Ads claim 30 conversions and creators claim 20, but overlap is unknown; do not sum to 50. Week 1 has 1,000 visits and week 2 has 400, a 60% decline in visits. The 400 sustained visits need comparison to the pre-launch baseline before a failure verdict.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given creator posts are still unpublished, exclude their forecast from actual results and keep their lane open.

2. Given 8 messages come from 2 customers, report 2 contributors and 8 messages, not 8 dissatisfied buyers.
