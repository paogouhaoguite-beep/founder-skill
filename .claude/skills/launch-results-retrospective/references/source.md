## Specific changes

A launch retrospective with receipt-qualified channel outcomes, feedback themes, sustained-demand checks and next-cycle decisions.

- Reconcile which launch actions actually occurred and when; keep partial or unknown outcomes visible before judging performance.
- Compare predeclared targets with actuals in the same windows and attribution basis. Label reconstructed targets and separate platform reach from store orders.
- Measure initial spike, sustained visits and owned-audience capture independently. Later repeat purchase needs a mature observation window rather than a launch-day estimate.
- Cluster feedback by underlying need, count unique contributors and preserve representative quotes. Separate prevalence from severity and route product decisions to owners.
- Investigate the largest material miss with an evidence-backed chain of explanations. Keep platform-algorithm stories as hypotheses and compare against a relevant baseline.
- Issue keep/change/stop recommendations with effort, owner and next read date. Turn resolved feedback into factual follow-up drafts; do not announce an improvement before it exists.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
