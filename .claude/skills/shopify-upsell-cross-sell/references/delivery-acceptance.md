# Delivery example and acceptance

Illustrative pairing: a real compatible accessory accompanies a real main product. Show both IDs, compatibility evidence, actual/proposed bundle price with its status, margin calculation completeness, placement, final recommendation copy and inventory exclusions. A curated recommendation says "Pair with" rather than "Customers also bought" when no basket evidence exists.

Acceptance: exact real products, useful compatibility, contribution impact, finished copy, feasible placement and measurable guardrails. No fake basket evidence, unsupported checkout capability or analysis-only discount creation.
