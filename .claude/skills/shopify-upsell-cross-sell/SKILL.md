# Relevant bundles and product recommendations

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=shopify-upsell-cross-sell&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Read the real catalog, stock, prices, merchant rules and authorized order evidence. Propose compatible upgrades or complements based on product function, occasion and actual basket patterns. Do not claim “customers also bought” without purchase evidence; use a neutral label for a curated pairing. Do not fabricate review proof or scarcity.

Judge a bundle by pre-ad contribution, fulfillment/returns, available inventory and buyer utility, not only AOV. Use the store's price bands and margin floor; no universal 25–40% price ratio or guaranteed uplift. Provide product IDs, placement, finished copy, bundle price if authorized or a labeled proposal, discount cost and eligibility/exclusion rules.

Inspect the actual theme/app/checkout capabilities before promising one-click post-purchase upsell. A plan name alone does not prove support. If unsupported, deliver a product/cart recommendation preview or an opted-in follow-up draft as appropriate. Do not create discounts or send email as part of analysis without authorization.

For requested implementation, preview exact fields and eligible products, execute through connected tools, and verify prices, compatibility, inventory and customer path. Measure incremental contribution/order, attachment rate and checkout completion with a stated baseline. Save results and a bounded review plan; no automatic recurring task unless requested.

Before final delivery, read [delivery example and acceptance](references/delivery-acceptance.md).
