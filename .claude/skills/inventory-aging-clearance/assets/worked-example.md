# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

100 synthetic units cost USD 12 each. A markdown may sell all at USD 18 with USD 5 remaining costs: future recovery USD 1,300; historical full margin USD 100. Liquidation at USD 9 less USD 1 costs recovers USD 800 and has a USD 400 historical loss. The markdown is preferable only if the assumed volume and timing are credible; neither USD 1,300 nor USD 800 is profit.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

With reliable lot dates, separate the two cash recoveries from the full-cost margins and report the sales assumption.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

With no receipts, label age unavailable and offer a cover-based screen without inventing first-receipt dates or classifying units as expired.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
