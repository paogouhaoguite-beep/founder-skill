# Transcript Derived Content Package

## Inputs and access
Require media or authorized transcript, language, speaker labels supplied by the merchant, reuse rights and target outputs. Transcription tools are optional; supplied transcript text supports editing and derivatives, but not audio verification. Do not identify an unknown person from voice or invent timestamps.

## Transcript evidence
Record source/version/duration. Prefer actual audio or official captions where available, distinguishing auto-caption text from verified transcription. Mark inaudible words, uncertain names and speaker ambiguity. Check product names, numbers and claims against the recording and authorized facts. Preserve the original before cleanup.

Build topic segments with time anchors actually observed. Summarize the episode’s concrete question, main points and limitations without strengthening claims. Show notes should link named resources only when verified. Titles and descriptions reflect actual content; do not promise a topic the episode never covers.

Create derivative quote cards, article outlines or clip suggestions from specific transcript spans. Keep exact quotes exact; label edited paraphrases. Preserve surrounding conditions and avoid turning a guest opinion into a product guarantee. Search optimization uses audience language and actual topics, not keyword stuffing or ranking promises.

## Deliver
Return transcript with uncertainty markers, segment table, finished summary/show notes, proposed derivative excerpts and source spans. Without audio access mark timing and word accuracy unverified. Publishing or reuse beyond consent is separate.
