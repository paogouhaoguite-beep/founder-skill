# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic SKU A produces USD 10,000 net revenue, USD 9,000 variable costs and USD 5,000 average inventory: USD 1,000 contribution and 20% period contribution/inventory. B produces USD 4,000 revenue, USD 2,000 costs and USD 1,000 inventory: USD 2,000 and 200%. A leads revenue but B leads these contribution measures; this alone does not prove A should be removed.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Rank B above A on contribution and stock productivity while retaining the revenue ranking and period labels.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

For a low-selling replacement part required by existing customers, retain its service role and propose an explicit availability decision instead of automatic deletion.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
