# Press Outreach Kit

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a product press kit with beat-matched pitch drafts, fact sheet, evidence links and embargo-aware outreach tracker.

## Merchant inputs

Actual news event; product facts; evidence; press assets and rights; relevant outlets/journalists; launch timing; proposed embargo/exclusive terms.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Define the news value for the outlet’s audience, distinguishing a useful product story from an unsupported claim that the launch is revolutionary.

2. Build a small beat-matched contact list from recent relevant work, with source/date and a specific angle for each. Do not infer contact permission from a scraped email alone.

3. Prepare a concise pitch, factual release, FAQ, approved images and contact route. Substantiate comparisons, availability, pricing and customer quotations.

4. Separate exclusive offers from embargo agreements. A timestamp in a pitch does not mean a journalist accepted an embargo.

5. Plan follow-up and sample logistics based on stated publication lead times and relationship; keep no-response and declined contacts out of repeated pressure.

6. Measure delivered pitches, responses, actual coverage and observed referral traffic separately. Circulation, impressions and equivalent ad value do not prove attributable sales.

## Deliverable

A product press kit with beat-matched pitch drafts, fact sheet, evidence links and embargo-aware outreach tracker.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
