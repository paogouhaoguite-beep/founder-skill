# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Merchant launches a repair kit on 15 October at $18; replacement-part testing is documented. Draft subject: “Repair kit for replaceable bag hardware — available 15 October”. Draft pitch: “Hello, your recent repair guides cover how owners can keep bags in use. Our $18 repair kit launches on 15 October and includes replaceable hardware tested on the models listed in the fact sheet. I can provide the test notes and approved images if useful for your coverage. Would you like the kit details?” This is a draft, not sent; avoid “world first”. Three journalists are contacted; one accepts an embargo and one only acknowledges receipt. Expected embargo table marks only the accepting journalist as agreed. Two published URLs count as 2 placements, not their estimated readership as customers.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given a journalist has not accepted an embargo, do not describe the information as contractually protected.

2. Given a press image includes an unlicensed customer face, keep it out of the distributable kit.
