## Specific changes

A product press kit with beat-matched pitch drafts, fact sheet, evidence links and embargo-aware outreach tracker.

- Define the news value for the outlet’s audience, distinguishing a useful product story from an unsupported claim that the launch is revolutionary.
- Build a small beat-matched contact list from recent relevant work, with source/date and a specific angle for each. Do not infer contact permission from a scraped email alone.
- Prepare a concise pitch, factual release, FAQ, approved images and contact route. Substantiate comparisons, availability, pricing and customer quotations.
- Separate exclusive offers from embargo agreements. A timestamp in a pitch does not mean a journalist accepted an embargo.
- Plan follow-up and sample logistics based on stated publication lead times and relationship; keep no-response and declined contacts out of repeated pressure.
- Measure delivered pitches, responses, actual coverage and observed referral traffic separately. Circulation, impressions and equivalent ad value do not prove attributable sales.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
