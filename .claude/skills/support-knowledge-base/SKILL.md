# Support Knowledge Base

Built by [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=support-knowledge-base&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) for independent ecommerce and DTC operators. This skill works without a ShopChief account.

## Start from the actual task

Approved policy/product/care documents with dates and markets; common ticket themes; existing articles and search terms; content owner; known exceptions and revision schedule.

Use supplied exports, documents and merchant facts first. Reuse known context and ask only for decision-critical omissions. An authorized connector can supply current records after verifying the account, schema and scope; none is bundled. Without tools, finish a bounded analysis or draft from the supplied evidence and identify the missing inputs. Unknown amounts are not zero. Keep dates, units, currency and observed versus assumed values explicit.

## Decision workflow

1. Cluster questions by customer task and country/product applicability, not just keyword similarity. Detect conflicting policies before drafting an answer; the current marketing page alone may not describe the policy applicable to an older purchase.
2. For each answer retain the supporting source/version, effective dates and exceptions. Distinguish operational guidance from legal rights and require current official evidence where needed. Avoid placing customer names, order details or internal tokens in public articles.
3. Write the direct answer, prerequisites, concrete steps, expected outcome, troubleshooting and handoff when the evidence ends. Link to relevant store destinations supplied by the merchant; do not invent live URLs. Reuse approved policy language where authorized rather than extending commitments.
4. Review coverage by actual question frequency and unresolved intent. A knowledge article can be drafted without a helpdesk; publishing or enabling an automated answer policy requires the corresponding scope and checking the live result.

## Deliverable

Return the completed calculation or decision record, not just instructions to do it. Use this task-specific table, supplemented by actual drafts or a calculation bridge when needed:

| Article/question | product/market | final answer | source/version | exception/escalation | owner | review trigger | draft/published |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include sources and observation dates, blocked decisions, an owner and the next verification step. Save only in the authorized working folder or actual client artifact tool and report the real path; inline delivery is valid when persistence is unavailable. Do not require another skill, a proprietary UI or an invented tool. External changes, payments, spending and messages require the corresponding user scope; preparation alone does not authorize them.
