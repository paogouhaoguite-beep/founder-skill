**Title: How should I clean my pouch?**

**Applies to:** The pouch covered by product specification CARE-02, revision 2.

Wipe your pouch clean. This is the cleaning method in the approved product instructions.

Machine washing has not been verified for this pouch. We also do not have approved guidance for detergents, soaking, drying temperatures or stain treatments, so we cannot recommend those methods here.

If wiping does not address the mark, contact support with the product name and a description of the mark. A photo can help if you can provide one without showing personal information. Support will check the product instructions before suggesting another method.

If the zip is broken, contact support with your order reference and a description of the problem. This article does not determine warranty or replacement eligibility.
