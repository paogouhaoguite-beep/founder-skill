# Product Video Localization

Produce localized video files from an existing approved product video. This owns language-specific audio, captions, visual copy and exports, rather than creating a new ad concept.

## Establish the source of truth

Collect the source video, transcript if available, target languages/markets, editable layers or textless master, product terminology, prices/units/offer terms and voice permissions. Transcribe what is actually audible and inventory visible text. Preserve SKU, conditions and material qualifications. Flag a source claim that lacks evidence rather than making it stronger in translation.

Separate translation from offer localization. Converting a currency, price, shipping promise, measurement or promotion date can change the commercial meaning and requires supplied market facts. Keep brand/product names according to the approved glossary. If there is no clean background under baked-in text, do not pretend to remove it perfectly; use a verified edit, suitable text panel or a disclosed limitation.

## Produce each language

An unconfirmed discount blocks that claim, not all production: omit it from speech and pixels while continuing with confirmed market facts. When the target duration changes, re-edit source footage, verified still frames and legible information cards as appropriate; an existing editor can do this without a generative video tool. Preserve the true order and speed of product actions, and label planned timing until actual audio is measured.

Read [production execution](references/production.md). Translate by meaning and actual shot function, then record/synthesize an authorized voice or create subtitles only if that is the requested scope. Never clone a real person's voice without permission. Check that the selected tool supports the language and needed pronunciation; a general speech capability does not prove dubbing or lip-sync support.

Measure the resulting audio files. Re-time caption cues to actual spoken words; revise translation, pauses or permitted visual holds when timing differs. Do not derive final captions from estimated word counts. Where on-camera speech is retained, either use verified authorized lip-sync, clearly chosen voice-over treatment or subtitles; do not silently ship mismatched lips as a completed dub.

Execute the available audio, caption, overlay and rendering steps within existing authorization. Review each locale independently for script accuracy, audible pronunciation, clipping, subtitle boundaries, obscured product detail and unchanged legal/offer qualifiers. Save locale-specific versions, not repeated copies of the source.

## Deliver

Return per-language video files, separate caption files and audio tracks when requested, glossary, translation/cue table and measured export details. Report which languages were actually rendered versus translation-only. Without production tools, deliver full translated scripts and an explicit timing handoff with `audio/video not produced`; proposed timing must be labeled provisional.
