## Specific changes

A creator outreach draft sequence with evidence-based personalization, stop conditions and negotiation pipeline.

- Validate that each creator is a relevant prospect and that contact details and eligibility come from permitted evidence; keep personal details out of durable reports.
- Personalize from actual work with a source/date, explaining why this creator’s audience and format fit the product occasion. Never invent a compliment or shared relationship.
- Draft a concise offer stating sender, product, deliverable idea, compensation basis and a low-pressure next step. Distinguish gifting without obligation from a paid requirement.
- Choose follow-up timing from the relationship and campaign deadline, cap touches and stop on refusal or opt-out. There is no mandatory universal cadence.
- Prepare scope-versus-price alternatives without promising future paid work as guaranteed value. Record proposed, replied, negotiating and agreed stages separately.
- Before any authorized send, verify exact recipient, final payload and current suppression state. Retain actual send/reply evidence; a CRM stage is not proof of delivery.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
