# Creator Outreach Workflow

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a creator outreach draft sequence with evidence-based personalization, stop conditions and negotiation pipeline.

## Merchant inputs

Creator shortlist; dated work examples; campaign fit; contact eligibility; prior outreach/suppression; compensation range; proposed deliverables and sender identity.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Validate that each creator is a relevant prospect and that contact details and eligibility come from permitted evidence; keep personal details out of durable reports.

2. Personalize from actual work with a source/date, explaining why this creator’s audience and format fit the product occasion. Never invent a compliment or shared relationship.

3. Draft a concise offer stating sender, product, deliverable idea, compensation basis and a low-pressure next step. Distinguish gifting without obligation from a paid requirement.

4. Choose follow-up timing from the relationship and campaign deadline, cap touches and stop on refusal or opt-out. There is no mandatory universal cadence.

5. Prepare scope-versus-price alternatives without promising future paid work as guaranteed value. Record proposed, replied, negotiating and agreed stages separately.

6. Before any authorized send, verify exact recipient, final payload and current suppression state. Retain actual send/reply evidence; a CRM stage is not proof of delivery.

## Deliverable

A creator outreach draft sequence with evidence-based personalization, stop conditions and negotiation pipeline.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
