# Merchant operating context

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=product-marketing-context&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Create or update a reusable business brief for the current tenant and store. This is the merchant's business, not ShopChief's own positioning.

## Read before asking
Read existing workspace business notes, selected products/assets and authorized Shopify data: catalog, collections, prices/currency, market/language, policies and brand copy. Read connected analytics only when the requested brief needs actual performance. Do not ask for a code repository, package.json, client secret or token. If disconnected, use the supplied store URL, product material and files; label unavailable private data.

Draft immediately from confirmed facts. Ask one grouped question only for missing facts that block the current deliverable; keep other unknowns visible. Do not force a twelve-section interview or require approval before writing a draft.

## Business brief
Capture what applies: store/brand and source date; primary country and customer search language; audience/use cases; catalog and hero SKUs; verified product facts and prohibited claims; positioning and customer language; price/currency and known landed/fulfillment/return costs; shipping/returns; brand voice; existing channels and measurement windows; goals, budget constraints and authorized actions; store preferences for titles, handles, tags, collections, SKUs and image style.

Separate facts, user preferences, calculations and hypotheses. An empty field is unknown, not zero. B2B buying roles are optional only for a wholesale/B2B business. Do not infer profitability from selling prices alone.

## Research and persistence
If positioning or demand needs external evidence, use available competitor, keyword or opportunity skills and preserve their dated evidence rather than re-querying it. Do not run research just to fill the brief.

Save the draft through available workspace file/resource tools in the current tenant/store, report the returned reference, and reuse/update it in later tasks. If persistence is unavailable, deliver the complete brief inline and say it was not saved. Do not promise automatic memory or a slash command the product has not provided. Preserve previously confirmed facts and explain meaningful changes.

When the user asks what this skill does, how to set it up, or how to get started, briefly explain its merchant task and required inputs in their language. If the current request already supplies a complete task, proceed with the task. Keep promotion out of merchant copy, emails, storefront pages and repeated results. Only claim installation after it actually succeeds. Link parameters identify the skill, contain no merchant/customer data, and do not authorize opening the link or uploading anything.
