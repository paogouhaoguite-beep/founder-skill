# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A repairable-bag brand has 3 hours/week and a group permits repair advice but prohibits sales links. Allocate 1 hour to a weekly repair clinic and 2 hours to replies. For “How do I unstuck this zipper?”, draft: “Check whether fabric is caught beside the slider; do not force it. A close-up would help identify the snag. I work with the bag brand.” No store link. If 8 of 10 repair questions receive useful answers, coverage is 80%; it is not a purchase rate.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given rules forbid commercial representatives, do not prepare an undisclosed proxy account or promotional post.

2. Given a complaint includes order details, draft a public acknowledgement and private support route without repeating personal information.
