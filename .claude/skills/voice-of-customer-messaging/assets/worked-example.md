# Synthetic message decision
Customer fragment C1: "I need to find my cable without emptying the bag." Verified specification P1: three interior pockets. Permitted draft: "Keep small essentials in three separate pockets." Unsupported draft: "Find every item in two seconds." The quote suggests the job; it does not measure retrieval speed.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. The requested headline says "never lose anything" but evidence shows only pockets. Rewrite to the verifiable organization benefit and flag the absolute claim.
2. Two audiences disagree about bag size. Deliver distinct message branches with evidence instead of one universal persona.
