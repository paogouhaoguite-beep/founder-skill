# Voice-of-customer messaging

> By [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=voice-of-customer-messaging&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) · Practical workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. No ShopChief account required.

## Separate language from proof
Collect the audience/use case, product facts, raw customer excerpts with source IDs, approved claims, brand voice and intended page/email/ad placement. Use provided assets offline; an authorized connector is optional. Missing customer evidence means a hypothesis-led draft, labeled as such, not invented customer language.

Create three columns before writing: exact customer language; interpretation of the underlying job/objection; product evidence that can answer it. An emotional quote establishes an experience, not a universal performance claim. Store names and contact details outside public copy. Obtain an appropriate publication basis before using an identifiable testimonial; otherwise paraphrase the theme without quotation marks or attribution.

## Find the message order
Distinguish the purchase trigger from desired benefit, comparison alternative, objection and proof requirement. Segment where buyers want conflicting outcomes: lightweight travel and maximum capacity may need different messages. Choose a primary audience from the actual brief, not the loudest review. Do not claim statistical validation from a handful of verbatims.

Write a headline/subheadline, benefit-to-proof blocks, objection responses and CTA for the selected placement. For every factual sentence retain a claim ledger with source, qualification and approval status. Prefer a precise physical property to a promise that the corpus cannot verify. Keep customer wording natural without reproducing long copyrighted passages.

## Deliver a testable message set
Return `message | audience/job | evidence IDs | product proof | uncertainty | placement`, followed by finished copy and a small set of materially different alternatives. Explain the decision each alternative tests. Define an evaluation task such as whether buyers can identify fit and contents from the page; if a conversion experiment is requested, specify eligible visitors, assigned variant, purchase/contribution outcome and observation window. Do not forecast a lift.

The deliverable is draft messaging and a claims ledger. Editing a live page, sending email, collecting new interviews or running paid research follows separately authorized scope.
