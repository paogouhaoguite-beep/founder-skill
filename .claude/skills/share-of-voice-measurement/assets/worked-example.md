# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Same forum/week: merchant 40 mentions, rival A 35, rival B 25. Share = 40/(40+35+25) = 40%. Next week a fourth rival adds 100 mentions; reporting 40/200 = 20% as a decline would change the panel. Show the original-panel 40% separately and start the expanded-panel series at 20%.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given every count is zero, report share undefined, not 0% or 100%.

2. Given rival B data is unavailable, withhold the full-panel share or label a clearly reduced panel; do not assume zero.
