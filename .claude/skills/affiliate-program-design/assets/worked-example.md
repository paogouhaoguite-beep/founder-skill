# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic order list value USD 100, discount USD 10: commissionable net merchandise revenue USD 90. Non-ad variable costs USD 54, commission 10% of 90 = USD 9, platform variable fee USD 2. Residual contribution = 90 − 54 − 9 − 2 = USD 25. The USD 10 discount is not deducted again. A later USD 30 merchandise refund reduces the commission basis to USD 60 and commission to USD 6; cost/refund economics must also be reconciled, not assumed proportional. Pay only the validated amount under the agreed hold policy.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the net USD 90 already includes a discount, produce USD 25 residual contribution without deducting the USD 10 twice.
2. Given a partial refund before payout, recalculate eligible commission and preserve an audit row rather than paying original commission silently.
