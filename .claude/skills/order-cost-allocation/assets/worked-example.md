# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic orders A/B have net sales 100/200, COGS40/80, direct fulfillment+fees 10/20. Contribution is 50/100. Shared marketing 30 allocated equally gives 15/15; overhead 60 by net sales gives 20/40. Allocated results are **15/45**, total 60. Controls: sales 300−COGS120−variable 30−marketing 30−overhead 60=60. Equal overhead 30/30 changes results to 5/55 but not total 60.

| Order | Contribution | Marketing | Revenue-share overhead | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 50 | 15 | 20 | 15 |
| B | 100 | 15 | 40 | 45 |

## Boundary case 1

Refund-only period makes net-revenue allocation denominator zero. Do not divide or force negative shares; retain unallocated pool and select a supported alternative driver.

## Boundary case 2

Label L1 costs 9 and order A has 3 lines. Allocate 9 across lines once; do not join 9 to all 3 and report 27.
