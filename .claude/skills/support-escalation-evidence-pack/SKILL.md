# Support Escalation Evidence Pack

## Intake
Use ticket IDs, timeline, order/SKU identifiers, screenshots or logs, relevant policies, tried actions and the receiving team’s scope. Work locally without connectors by producing a draft with links to supplied evidence. Do not delay a urgent safety escalation merely because some fields are missing.

## Build the escalation
Separate customer-reported symptoms from verified observations. State what fails, who is affected, since when and what is still unknown. Quantify known affected orders without extrapolating ticket volume into total incidence. Revenue exposure is not realized loss. Set severity using the merchant’s actual impact and policy, not a SaaS ARR table.

For a storefront problem, include exact steps, device/browser, expected versus actual result and reproducibility. For a physical-product or fulfillment problem, include SKU/lot/order, relevant dates and observed condition. Preserve the evidence chain and redact unnecessary personal information. Do not run invasive diagnostics or request unsafe customer tests.

List every attempted remedy and its outcome, a verified workaround if any, then a single specific ask: decide an exception, inspect a lot, investigate a failure or provide a supported workaround. Name an owner and a justified deadline or explicitly unknown deadline. Keep customer communication ownership even when the technical issue moves teams.

## Deliver
Return a paste-ready internal escalation with headline, impact, chronology, reproduction/evidence, actions tried, current workaround, ask and next update plan. Sending it or notifying a customer is separately scoped. Do not promise root cause or closure until the investigation and remedy are verified.
