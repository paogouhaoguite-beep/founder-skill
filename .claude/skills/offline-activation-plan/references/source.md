## Specific changes

An offline-to-store activation plan with venue/creative constraints, staffing economics and attribution limits.

- Define the customer experience and online-store handoff for a pop-up, demo or out-of-home placement. Choose formats by actual occasion, not novelty.
- Inventory venue/media, staffing, stock, transport, insurance/permissions and production lead times. Distinguish a booked commitment from a proposed line item.
- Specify the physical creative or demonstration with readable distance/attention constraints and accurate product claims. Validate venue/platform specifications before production.
- Design trackable QR/code/landing paths plus optional voluntary research; acknowledge people may see the placement and purchase later without the code.
- Create cost and capacity scenarios, an incident/stockout plan and a stop condition. Do not infer incremental sales from attributed QR scans alone.
- Deliver an activation brief, schedule, cost ledger and evidence plan; bookings, invitations and purchases remain separate authorized actions.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
