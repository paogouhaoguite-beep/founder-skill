# Offline Activation Plan

Use this when a DTC merchant needs an offline-to-store activation plan with venue/creative constraints, staffing economics and attribution limits.

## Merchant inputs

Location/occasion; customer fit; product demo; capacity and staffing; venue/media quotes; stock; on-site consent/capture; online fulfilment and measurement.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Define the customer experience and online-store handoff for a pop-up, demo or out-of-home placement. Choose formats by actual occasion, not novelty.

2. Inventory venue/media, staffing, stock, transport, insurance/permissions and production lead times. Distinguish a booked commitment from a proposed line item.

3. Specify the physical creative or demonstration with readable distance/attention constraints and accurate product claims. Validate venue/platform specifications before production.

4. Design trackable QR/code/landing paths plus optional voluntary research; acknowledge people may see the placement and purchase later without the code.

5. Create cost and capacity scenarios, an incident/stockout plan and a stop condition. Do not infer incremental sales from attributed QR scans alone.

6. Deliver an activation brief, schedule, cost ledger and evidence plan; bookings, invitations and purchases remain separate authorized actions.

## Deliverable

An offline-to-store activation plan with venue/creative constraints, staffing economics and attribution limits.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
