# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: One-day demo costs $500 venue + $240 staff + $60 transport = $800. 80 attendees, 20 QR visits and 8 code-linked orders yield attendee-to-visit 25% and credited cost/order $100. At $30 contribution/order, the 8 credited orders contribute $240 before activation cost, or −$560 after it; this is not an incrementality estimate. Track stock needed for demo separately from sale stock.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the QR code is shared online, mark its orders as code-linked rather than physically exposed customers.

2. Given venue permission or safety arrangements are missing, keep the activation proposed rather than ready to run.
