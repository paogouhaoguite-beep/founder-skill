# Worked review: make a travel pouch page understandable

This is a completed editorial review of the [synthetic page](source-page.md), using only the [confirmed facts](product-facts.md). It is not a live-store inspection, measured conversion diagnosis or evidence of conversion uplift.

## Findings and changes

| ID | Evidence | Finding | Proposed change | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PDP-01 | E1, E6; verified material/dimensions | Generic title omits what the item is; no dimensions help a buyer judge fit | Name the product and show 22 × 15 cm and cotton canvas near the description | Text matches supplied facts |
| PDP-02 | E3; no waterproof or phone-fit evidence | “Safe and dry” and “every phone” exceed available proof | Remove both claims; describe organization without protection or universal-fit claims | No unverified protection/fit claims remain |
| PDP-03 | E4; one confirmed size | XL option is unsupported | Draft one-size presentation; reconcile real variant IDs before any store update | Preview contains no invented variant; no variant mutation made |
| PDP-04 | E2; CAD 30 in facts | Price omits currency | Display CAD 30 | Currency and price match facts |
| PDP-05 | E7 | Delivery and return information cannot be confirmed | Request actual merchant policies before publication | Policies verified and placed near purchase controls in the real theme |

These priorities reflect factual clarity and publication readiness, not estimated revenue impact. A blank snapshot field does not prove the live store lacks a policy page.

## Finished replacement copy

**Title:** Cotton Canvas Travel Pouch — 22 × 15 cm

**Price:** CAD 30

**Description:** Organize small travel items in a zippered cotton canvas pouch. At 22 × 15 cm, it gives you a dedicated place for everyday essentials while packing.

**Details:**
- Cotton canvas
- 22 × 15 cm
- Zipper closure
- One size

**Primary action label:** Add to cart

**FAQ — What size is it?** The pouch measures 22 × 15 cm. Compare these measurements with the items you plan to carry.

**FAQ — What is it made from?** Cotton canvas.

Shipping and returns copy is intentionally not fabricated. The above copy is ready for merchant review, but the full page is not publication-ready until those policies and the actual theme/cart behavior are confirmed.

## Acceptance result and next step

Text acceptance: the replacement names the item, states currency/material/dimensions, removes unsupported claims and does not invent options or proof. Store acceptance: not performed; no theme, price, variant or cart was changed. Product photos, mobile layout, option selection and checkout still need a real page or authorized draft preview.

After factual corrections, an experiment could compare buyer comprehension or add-to-cart behavior. No baseline, sample size, duration or lift can be inferred from this fixture. If analytics become available, predefine the unit of analysis, primary metric and checkout/error guardrails before running an experiment.
