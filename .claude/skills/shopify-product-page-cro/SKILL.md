# Product-page conversion improvement

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/solutions/shopify-product-descriptions?utm_source=shopify-product-page-cro&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Inspect the actual product page on mobile and available catalog, analytics and merchant policies. Distinguish measured conversion/funnel data from a heuristic page review. Match period/device/traffic source; don't label a new store unhealthy from a generic conversion benchmark.

Check product identity/visual clarity, price and variant selection, purchase CTA, delivery/returns, genuine proof, readability and observable performance. Recommend only truthful claims: no invented ratings, live visitor counts, stock scarcity, delivery guarantees or policy promises. Prioritize the merchant's actual product and buyer objections; five images or a fixed urgency threshold are not mandatory.

If search intent mismatch may explain poor conversion, reuse DataForSEO keyword/SERP evidence under [query contract](references/dataforseo-contract.md). External keyword data cannot establish the store's conversion rate. For performance distinguish lab and field data; no universal revenue loss per extra second.

Deliver per-page findings with evidence, severity and specific replacement copy/layout/asset instructions. Use available copywriting/image tools for requested finished assets. Propose a measurable experiment only when traffic/sample size can support it, with primary metric and guardrails such as contribution/order and checkout completion. For small samples use a bounded before/after observation and label causal uncertainty.

Preview concrete writes and use connected tools within authorization; read back and inspect results. Save baseline and next check. If checkout, analytics or theme access is unavailable, state the gap and complete the supported work without routing to nonexistent skills.

Before final delivery, read [delivery example and acceptance](references/delivery-acceptance.md).
