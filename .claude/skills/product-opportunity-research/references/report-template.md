# Product decision brief

1. Target: Shopify, requested market, product/category, constraints and requested period.
2. Verdict: test / defer / reject, or insufficient evidence to decide; explain the deciding evidence.
3. Relevant candidates: identity, source links, observed prices and available estimates; report actual source platform, country and period beside metrics.
4. Compare supported demand, competition, price space, supply and risk. List missing inputs and disconfirming evidence. Do not force a winner or invent a composite score.
5. Next validation: measurable checks and stop conditions. Financial scenarios explicitly separate quotes, fees and assumptions; missing costs do not become zero.
6. Evidence handoff: source URLs, report month, retrieval time, sample scope and limitations; save with available workspace tools only when supported.
