# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: 200 direct sessions include 80 on long product URLs. Of 20 optional survey respondents, 6 mention a friend’s message. Expected report: 80/200 = 40% deep-URL direct share and 6/20 = 30% message-reported share among respondents. Neither proves 30% or 40% of all sales came from dark social. Do not add 80 + 6 as unique customers.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given a QR campaign intentionally lands on the deep URL, account for it before interpreting the direct segment.

2. Given only satisfied customers answered the survey, state response bias and avoid population extrapolation.
