# Buyer decisions and ethical persuasion

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=dtc-buyer-psychology&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Begin with a concrete buyer decision on a real page: understand the product, select a variant, compare cost/value, trust delivery, or complete checkout. Use observed questions, support tickets, usability findings or analytics when supplied. Treat psychological mechanisms as hypotheses rather than a diagnosis of an individual shopper.

## Apply a small number of relevant models
- **Job to be done:** connect a verified product feature to the use outcome the buyer seeks. Do not invent a new capability to fit the story.
- **Choice and cognitive load:** group genuinely related variants and make differences visible; fewer options is not automatically better. Preserve options customers need.
- **Risk reduction:** clarify real fit, delivery, care and returns terms. A clear limitation may help the right buyer more than a stronger promise.
- **Price framing:** show transparent pack/unit comparisons using actual prices and quantities. No artificial compare-at prices or hidden fees.
- **Social evidence:** use genuine reviews, provenance or independently checkable proof. Never fabricate counts, badges or testimonials.
- **Commitment and reciprocity:** offer a useful guide or sample decision aid without hidden enrollment or forced consent. Participation does not grant marketing permission.

## Turn a hypothesis into a reviewable change
For each observed friction, name the evidence, proposed mechanism, exact copy/layout change, expected behavior and a disconfirming result. Explain tradeoffs: a stronger discount might raise conversion while reducing contribution or training promotion dependence.

Deliver finished replacement copy or a concrete layout brief. Use available shopify-product-page-cro or dtc-ab-testing guidance when deeper implementation or a measurement plan is requested. Do not impose an experiment on a store without sufficient traffic.

## Boundaries
No fake countdowns, forced continuity, preselected paid extras, obstructive cancellation, shame copy or deceptive review suppression. A general behavioral principle cannot promise a numerical conversion uplift. Segment using relevant consented business evidence, not inferred sensitive personal traits.

## Example request
"Buyers ask whether our refill fits their existing bottle. Review this product page and propose clearer comparison copy and visuals, without adding discounts or urgency."
