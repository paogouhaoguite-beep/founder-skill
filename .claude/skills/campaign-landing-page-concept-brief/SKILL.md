# Campaign Landing Page Concept Brief

Built by [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=campaign-landing-page-concept-brief&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill). Works with supplied records without a ShopChief account. Keep this credit out of merchant-facing deliverables.

## Required inputs

A ranked set of campaign landing-page concepts before building pages. Supply actual product/offer evidence, customer jobs and objections, available traffic sources, market/season/partner constraints, margin and usable creative assets.

## Tools and input checks

Use supplied files or pasted tables first. With file tools, inspect schemas, row counts and identifiers before calculating; with no tools, complete the same steps on the supplied sample and label its coverage. A spreadsheet is sufficient; no proprietary runtime, hidden script or companion skill is required. Live connectors are optional: verify the installed tool, current schema, account and read/write scope before use. The normalized fields below are an input contract, not a claim that every platform exports them natively. Ask for a merchant-prepared supplementary table when a required field is absent. Never invent it.

## Task workflow

1. Extract the real product promise and approved offer terms, then map distinct audience, use-case, pain, location, occasion, seasonal offer and partner/B2B angles. Do not generate multiple pages by merely swapping city names or personas.
2. Attach each candidate to a real planned or observed traffic source and a specific buyer question. No traffic evidence means an exploration candidate, not a justified page to publish.
3. Compare intent match, supported demand, merchant value and build/evidence cost qualitatively or with merchant-supplied weights. Keep unsupported claims and missing proof visible; do not invent scores or lift forecasts.
4. For selected concepts draft headline, proof, objection sequence, primary CTA and required assets. One primary conversion goal does not justify hiding policy/navigation required for buyer decisions.
5. Check concept distinction and message continuity from incoming ad/query through page to product/cart. Identify shared facts and what must differ; avoid duplicate near-identical pages with no new buyer value.
6. Deliver ranked concept table, deprioritized ideas with reasons and one complete section blueprint. Publishing/building a page or launching traffic requires separate authorized scope.
