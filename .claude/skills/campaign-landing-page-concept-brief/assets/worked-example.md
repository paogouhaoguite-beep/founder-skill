# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic product: compact insulated lunch bag, confirmed dimensions and reusable liner; no leakproof claim. Existing search campaign targets “small work lunch bag”; planned creator video shows packing a lunch. Rank 1 workday-fit page: headline “Pack your work lunch in a compact bag”; proof dimension diagram and actual packed-item photo; sections fit→liner care→shipping/returns→CTA “Choose your color.” Rank 2 creator packing page uses the actual demonstrated items/video with permission. Deprioritize “never leaks” angle: no supporting test. Do not create ten city variants because delivery is identical.
