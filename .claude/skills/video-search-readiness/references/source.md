## Specific changes

A product-video search readiness brief with watch-page context, truthful metadata, chapters and access checks.

- Distinguish a dedicated watch page from a product page with supplementary video; the search task and expected indexing behavior differ.
- Ensure the title and description describe the actual demonstration, product and viewing purpose. Avoid clickbait or keyword repetition that misstates content.
- Check that video and thumbnail are accessible and that essential context exists as readable text; transcripts should reflect the media rather than invented dialogue.
- Map valid video metadata, duration, publication date and chapters to the real asset. Do not claim structured data guarantees a video result.
- Review thumbnail clarity and chapter boundaries against actual playback; verify platform-specific metadata limits at implementation time.
- Deliver a video/page evidence checklist with exact metadata proposals and indexing observation plan. Separate uploaded, crawlable and observed-in-search states.
