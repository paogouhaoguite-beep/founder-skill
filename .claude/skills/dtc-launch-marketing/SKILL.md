# DTC launch marketing

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=dtc-launch-marketing&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Start from the actual product, buyer, target countries, ready inventory, fulfillment dates, price, contribution margin, returns policy, assets and authorized budget. Distinguish a new product, restock and seasonal collection. Do not apply a software beta or Product Hunt launch template to a physical-goods store.

## Readiness determines the promise
Record the product/variant facts, stock coverage, working PDP and checkout, shipping/return terms, support capacity and tracking. For a preorder, explicitly distinguish estimated dispatch from delivery and identify unresolved supply constraints. Do not manufacture scarcity, waitlist size, testimonials or launch sales.

## Choose distribution
Use an owned destination such as a PDP, collection or opted-in launch page. Select external social/search/creator channels based on audience fit and available assets. Define which useful demonstration each channel receives and how a buyer reaches the destination. Creator sampling, compensation, content rights, disclosures and tracking links need explicit commercial terms; a launch plan does not authorize outreach or a purchase.

Build phases appropriate to the launch: preparation, demand capture when justified, launch window, fulfillment/support and post-launch review. A restock may only need a back-in-stock notice and inventory checks. Explain dependencies and an owner for each action rather than forcing a fixed countdown.

## Commercial controls
Set a contribution-aware spend ceiling using confirmed variable costs, inventory and uncertainty; avoid guaranteeing ROAS. Discounts need an actual approved price, eligibility and margin impact. Include stop/hold conditions for broken checkout, inaccurate availability, fulfillment overload or a materially wrong claim.

## Deliverable
A dated launch plan with channel, audience, asset/copy, destination, owner, cost estimate, prerequisite, tracking event and completion evidence. Supply the requested launch copy and creator brief. Keep prepared, scheduled, sent, published and fulfilled as distinct states.

Readout distinguishes attributable link traffic, assisted conversions, total store orders, cancellations/returns and contribution. A before/after launch spike does not isolate a channel's incremental effect.

## Example request
"Prepare a launch plan for 300 units of a new travel pouch. We have an opted-in email list and two creator relationships. Include assets, fulfillment checks and a spend ceiling; do not send messages."
