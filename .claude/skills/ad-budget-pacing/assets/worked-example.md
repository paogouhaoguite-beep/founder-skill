# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic October media cap USD 3,000. Through 10 completed days, spend is USD 1,100; lag reserve USD 100; an unpaid placement outside that spend is USD 200. Remaining allocatable media = 3,000 − 1,100 − 100 − 200 = USD 1,600 across 21 days, or USD 76.19/day before rounding. A weighted plan gives four promotional days weight 2 and the other 17 weight 1: total weight 25, so promotion days get USD 128 and ordinary days USD 64. Total 4×128 + 17×64 = 1,600.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the placement cost is already included in the USD 1,100, do not subtract it again; explicitly reconcile the commitment status.
2. Given spent plus reserves exceeds the cap, report the gap and a pause/review proposal; never output a negative executable budget.
