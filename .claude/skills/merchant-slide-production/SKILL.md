# Merchant Slide Production

## Inputs and output mode
Collect audience, decision, source material, template/brand assets, duration, slide format and editability needs. Use available presentation tools; a complete slide-by-slide storyboard and notes is the fallback. Image-only slides are not editable charts/text, and a rendered preview is not a saved deck.

## Narrative and assembly
Write the one-sentence takeaway and an outline before styling. Each slide has one job, supporting evidence, visual form and presenter note. Cut unsupported claims and separate source facts from illustrative scenarios. Preserve an approved template’s layout roles and placeholders; inspect the template rather than assume every page is interchangeable.

Build using native editable text/tables/charts where required. Keep units, sources and qualifiers visible. Use supplied/licensed assets and verify correct product variants. Check title/body hierarchy, alignment, readable density, contrast, crop and logo proportions across all pages.

Plan timing from actual content and a measured speaking pace, allowing transitions, pauses and discussion. Do not divide total time equally when slides need different attention. For notes-only timing requests, change notes only; if the duration cannot fit, flag the mismatch rather than silently delete visible material.

Render every slide and review overflow, clipping, fonts, charts and speaker-note attachment. Open the target application when available and report any untested compatibility. Preserve original deck and inspect the final exported artifact.

## Deliver
Return actual deck or complete storyboard, notes/timing table, sources, editability and QA status. Do not publish, share or claim a presentation was rehearsed unless it was.
