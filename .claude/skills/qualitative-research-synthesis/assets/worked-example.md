# Synthetic worked example

Input: Three synthetic sessions about size selection. P1: “I measured my old bag.” P2: “I could not tell if my laptop fit.” P3: “The dimensions were enough; I checked them against my laptop.” All three were asked about sizing.

| Theme | Coverage | Evidence | Contrary case | Decision |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Shoppers compare dimensions to an owned item | 2/3 | P1 and P3 | P2 did not identify a comparison method | Test a dimension diagram next to the specification |
| Current information left fit unresolved | 1/3 | P2 quote | P3 resolved fit | Investigate how shoppers find dimensions before rewriting all copy |

Memo: This convenience sample supports a diagram prototype, not “67% of customers need better sizing.” The mechanism for P2’s difficulty is unresolved.

Boundary scenario 1: One participant supplies ten repeated complaints. Count one participant for coverage and retain multiple episode references separately.
Boundary scenario 2: A paraphrased note says “seemed confused.” Label it observer interpretation and exclude it from verbatim quotations.
