# Qualitative Research Synthesis

## Evidence intake
Require original research questions, dated notes/transcripts, participant IDs, recruitment context and allowed reuse. A spreadsheet or Markdown table is enough. Without source notes, provide an intake template only. Remove personal identifiers from shared outputs; distinguish verbatim text from interviewer paraphrase.

## Two-pass synthesis
Read each session in full. Extract one observable statement or episode per row with participant ID, location/timestamp and observation type. Do not turn “looked for dimensions” into “is anxious” without evidence.

Pool observations and cluster by underlying behavior, need or constraint. Delay naming clusters until at least one regrouping pass; retain IDs even when hiding them during clustering. Reattach IDs and count distinct participants once per theme. Multiple quotes from the same person are not independent corroboration.

For each candidate theme, seek negative cases and alternative explanations. A rare but serious accessibility barrier can be actionable without being common. Write an insight as behavior, evidence-supported mechanism, tension and decision implication. Mark inferred mechanisms explicitly.

Report theme coverage as n/N interviewed participants in the relevant eligible subgroup, with field dates; do not label this a market prevalence estimate. Judge credibility from evidence directness, consistency, sampling gaps and contradictions, not a fixed interview count or “last three interviews” saturation rule. If new themes continued, state the discovery remains open.

## Readout
Deliver the observation ledger, codebook, theme/evidence/contradiction/implication table, unanswered questions and a short decision memo. Every recommendation names the evidence and a reversible next test. Distinguish “participants said”, “we observed” and “we infer”. Do not fabricate quotes or publish identifiable testimony.

## Public discussions and journey evidence
For a public discussion, retain post/comment IDs, parent-child reply links, timestamps, access window and retrieval gaps. Read each comment with its parent and later clarification; quoting a claim to refute it is not endorsement. Deduplicate copied passages and separate comment counts from unique authors. Code argument, evidence and rebuttal; votes and flair are neither verified expertise nor a representative opinion poll. Name the sentiment unit/denominator, retain mixed/unclear items and report deleted or unloaded comments. Public authors are not verified customers unless evidence establishes that relationship.

When synthesizing a journey, link each stage's reported/observed action, expectation, feeling and difficulty to actual source IDs. Keep measured behavior, self-report and analyst inference in separate fields. Triangulation requires independent relevant evidence; three sources or many repeated mentions do not mechanically confer confidence.
