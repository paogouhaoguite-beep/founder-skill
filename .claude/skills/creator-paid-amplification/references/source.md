## Specific changes

A rights-qualified creator amplification plan with source-to-edit mapping, landing-page continuity and capped test conditions.

- Check exact rights for paid use, account/identity, territory, dates, placements and edits before assessing performance.
- Evaluate candidate content using comparable organic exposure, hook clarity, proof, product fit and CTA. Organic success is a hypothesis for paid performance, not a guaranteed return.
- Choose creator-identity advertising, brand-account advertising or a permitted derivative format based on the grant. Do not treat permission to repost as permission to run an identity-based ad.
- Map any reused quote, clip or content atom back to the approved source and rights. Preserve context and avoid edits that change endorsement meaning.
- Check creator promise, code, price, variant and offer on the landing page. Prioritize the continuity gaps before spending traffic on a contradictory page.
- Design a capped paid test with declared economics and a read date, while logging asset/license expiry. Prepare activation settings; do not turn on spend or upload audiences without authorization.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions.
