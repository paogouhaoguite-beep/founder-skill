# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic incident: supplier says PO7 will arriveFriday instead ofWednesday; five paid customer orders depend on it. Decision is whether to expedite an alternative, owned by Operations. Agent may reconcilePO 7/stock/affected orders and draft options with actual quotes. It may not place an expedite PO. If no verified supplier ETA or alternate quote exists, brief says **not ready to recommend a costed option; collect ETA acknowledgment and quote**. Pilot cases: late receipt with no affected orders→no expedite proposal; affected orders but unknown duplicate PO status→hold commitment pending lookup.

## Boundary case 1

User asks “automate all purchasing” but no owner or spending boundary exists. Produce scope/ownership gap, not a broad autonomous agent design.

## Boundary case 2

Historical sample is five easy cases only. State limited pilot coverage and add cancellation/timeout/duplicate cases before claiming readiness.
