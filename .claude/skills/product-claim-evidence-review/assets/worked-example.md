# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic label says "100% recycled bottle, carbon neutral product". Certificate C1 covers bottle body with 80% post-consumer recycled plastic, excludes cap; no lifecycle or offset evidence.

| Claim | Finding | Proposed draft |
|---|---|---|
|100% recycled bottle|overbroad vs C1|"Bottle body contains 80% post-consumer recycled plastic; cap excluded" pending category/market review|
|carbon neutral product|no supporting scope/calculation|remove from proposed publication pending evidence; no invented substitute|

This is an evidence-gap result, not legal certification.

## Boundary case 1

Study tests ingredient at a different dose from finished SKU. Mark indirect evidence; do not claim the finished product clinically proven.

## Boundary case 2

A source describes a proposed environmental rule. Preserve proposal status and verify effective obligations; do not treat it as enacted law.
