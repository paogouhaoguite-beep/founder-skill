## Specific changes

A creator agreement review with clause-level issues, scoped rights schedule, compensation obligations and negotiation alternatives.

- Extract each obligation into a clause table; distinguish content production, permission to reuse content, creator-identity advertising and exclusivity.
- Describe exact usage by asset, channel, placement, territory, dates and edit rights. A broad “social use” phrase does not resolve paid advertising or a new derivative edit.
- Reconcile compensation with deliverable count, approval timing, expenses, payment triggers and cancellation. Surface contradictory dates or units before drafting counters.
- Check disclosure, third-party music/image/model rights, approval rounds and removal after expiry. Flag unresolved legal interpretation for the appropriate reviewer rather than declaring a clause enforceable.
- Draft commercially specific alternatives and the question each resolves; do not insert arbitrary standard fees, license duration or perpetual rights.
- Return a reference-safe rights schedule and open negotiation list. Signing, sending terms or granting account permissions is a separate authorized action.
- For customer-sourced photos maintain per-asset provenance, permission request/response evidence, permitted store/organic/paid placements, edit/expiry limits and withdrawal status. A hashtag or public post is not reuse permission. Unknown or withdrawn scope blocks that placement; verify third-party app capability and actual permission records rather than assuming an app grants rights automatically.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
