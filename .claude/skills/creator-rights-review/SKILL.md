# Creator Rights Review

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a creator agreement review with clause-level issues, scoped rights schedule, compensation obligations and negotiation alternatives.

## Merchant inputs

Proposed deliverables; creator terms; fees; organic/paid placements; term/territory; exclusivity; revision and cancellation terms; ownership and approval evidence.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Extract each obligation into a clause table; distinguish content production, permission to reuse content, creator-identity advertising and exclusivity.

2. Describe exact usage by asset, channel, placement, territory, dates and edit rights. A broad “social use” phrase does not resolve paid advertising or a new derivative edit.

3. Reconcile compensation with deliverable count, approval timing, expenses, payment triggers and cancellation. Surface contradictory dates or units before drafting counters.

4. Check disclosure, third-party music/image/model rights, approval rounds and removal after expiry. Flag unresolved legal interpretation for the appropriate reviewer rather than declaring a clause enforceable.

5. Draft commercially specific alternatives and the question each resolves; do not insert arbitrary standard fees, license duration or perpetual rights.

6. Return a reference-safe rights schedule and open negotiation list. Signing, sending terms or granting account permissions is a separate authorized action.

7. For customer-sourced photos maintain per-asset provenance, permission request/response evidence, permitted store/organic/paid placements, edit/expiry limits and withdrawal status. A hashtag or public post is not reuse permission. Unknown or withdrawn scope blocks that placement; verify third-party app capability and actual permission records rather than assuming an app grants rights automatically.

## Deliverable

A creator agreement review with clause-level issues, scoped rights schedule, compensation obligations and negotiation alternatives.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
