# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A customer permits an email quote: “The small pouch fits my commuter bag.” The merchant also tests 20 pouches; all are 18 cm wide. Expected library has two units: a customer fit story restricted to email, and a measured width claim for those 20 tested units. Short copy: “An 18 cm pouch for small essentials.” Reject “Loved by all commuters” and do not reuse the quote in a paid ad.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given customer permission is revoked, mark all variants using that quote unavailable and list known placements for review.

2. Given an outcome happened after purchase but other changes occurred, describe timing and reported experience without attributing causality to the product.
