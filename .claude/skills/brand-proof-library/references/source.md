## Specific changes

A reusable brand story and proof library with evidence scope, consent, approved variants and unresolved proof gaps.

- Inventory origin, founder, customer and product-proof stories. Break multi-claim material into units so permissions and facts can be checked independently.
- For each unit preserve situation, tension, action and observed outcome. Do not add invented customer adversity or a causal improvement absent from the source.
- Map the story to a message pillar and the exact claim it supports. A founder opinion can explain intent but cannot prove performance.
- Record measurement population, dates, product version, consent, quotation limits and approved channels. Keep a paraphrase distinguishable from a verbatim quote.
- Write reusable short and long variants that preserve scope. Where a market-change narrative is useful, date the evidence and avoid calling a change inevitable from one anecdote.
- Flag expired rights, stale specifications and pillars with no proof. Produce a reuse table that tells editors what can be said, with what evidence, in which context.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
