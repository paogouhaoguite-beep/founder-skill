# Brand Proof Library

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a reusable brand story and proof library with evidence scope, consent, approved variants and unresolved proof gaps.

## Merchant inputs

Approved message pillars; customer stories with permission; measurements and source dates; product scope; exact claims and intended placements.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Inventory origin, founder, customer and product-proof stories. Break multi-claim material into units so permissions and facts can be checked independently.

2. For each unit preserve situation, tension, action and observed outcome. Do not add invented customer adversity or a causal improvement absent from the source.

3. Map the story to a message pillar and the exact claim it supports. A founder opinion can explain intent but cannot prove performance.

4. Record measurement population, dates, product version, consent, quotation limits and approved channels. Keep a paraphrase distinguishable from a verbatim quote.

5. Write reusable short and long variants that preserve scope. Where a market-change narrative is useful, date the evidence and avoid calling a change inevitable from one anecdote.

6. Flag expired rights, stale specifications and pillars with no proof. Produce a reuse table that tells editors what can be said, with what evidence, in which context.

## Deliverable

A reusable brand story and proof library with evidence scope, consent, approved variants and unresolved proof gaps.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
