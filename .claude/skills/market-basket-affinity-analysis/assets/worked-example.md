# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic 100 eligible orders including singles: A appears 20, B30, both 10. Support 10%; A→B50%; B→A33.333%; lift 0.10/(0.20×0.30)=**1.6667**. If only multi-item orders were retained, denominators would change and the result would be biased for the intended universe. Pair is a candidate pending stock/compatibility, not a proven 66.67% incremental sales gain. Another pair with 1 co-purchase out of tiny marginals is kept as sparse evidence, not automatically winner.
