# Customer Testimonial Production

## Inputs
Require customer-supplied experience, product/order context if appropriate, recording and reuse permissions, planned channels and any incentives/material connections. A question guide and edit brief are the fallback without recording tools. Do not fabricate customers or generate a fictitious first-person endorsement.

## Collection and editing
Ask about concrete context, prior alternatives, actual use and observed outcome using neutral prompts. Avoid steering customers toward a desired growth number or claim. Record whether an outcome is a recollection, measured result or opinion, including dates and conditions.

Provide a simple recording brief: stable framing, clear audio, actual product and a quiet setting. Capture an honest continuous account before requesting optional pickups. Permission must cover the intended edit, clips, paid ads, voice/image and duration/territory where relevant; public review availability alone is not reuse permission.

Build a timecoded cut with exact quote spans and context. B-roll must depict the correct product and not manufacture a demonstration of claimed performance. Do not splice separate statements into a stronger claim. Offer the agreed approval/review process and keep a record of the approved version and permitted uses.

## Deliver
Return interview/recording brief, consent register, source-to-edit map, finished draft script or actual video status and missing evidence. Disclose incentives as required by current applicable rules and platform requirements; verify those before publication. Posting or ad activation remains separately scoped.
