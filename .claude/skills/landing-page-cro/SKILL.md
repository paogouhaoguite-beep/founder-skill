# Campaign landing-page CRO

> By [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=landing-page-cro&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) · Practical workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. No ShopChief account required.

## Start at the incoming promise
Require the landing page or supplied capture, actual ad/email/search promise, target audience, primary conversion and market. Read offer terms, inventory constraints, product proof and optional analytics for the same date/device/traffic cohort. Without page access use supplied evidence and mark interactive states unverified.

Compare the incoming claim to the first screen: same product, price basis, incentive eligibility and destination? Identify where the page asks the buyer to infer included items, size, shipping, expiry or next step. Evaluate the decision sequence: relevance → product/offer understanding → proof → objections → action. One primary goal does not justify removing legally or commercially necessary navigation and policy access.

## Produce changes with evidence
For each issue quote or capture the actual element, describe the blocked decision, draft the replacement and list required proof. Distinguish functional bugs from plausible persuasion hypotheses. Do not add urgency, guarantees, badges or popularity figures without facts. Trace CTA through its destination, variant/offer carryover and back navigation, stopping before unauthorized checkout or submission.

Return `location | observation | affected cohort | proposed change | supporting fact | expected mechanism | verification`. Include a revised hero and section order, not merely "improve copy." State the baseline as purchases or qualified conversions / eligible landing sessions within a fixed window; avoid mixing users, sessions and clicks. Contribution per eligible visitor is useful only when refunds and costs are available on a compatible basis.

Prioritize observed blockers before experiments. For a test proposal, define assignment unit, one primary outcome, guardrails, observation lag and overlapping promotions; do not predict a numerical lift. Draft changes may be prepared locally, but live publishing and experiment activation require user-authorized scope.
