# Synthetic message mismatch
An email promises a two-piece organizer set for USD 40. The landing hero shows one pouch and says "From USD 25"; CTA opens an unrelated collection. Proposed draft explicitly names both pieces and the USD 40 set, with its actual eligibility; CTA targets that set. This fixes message consistency, not a proven revenue uplift.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. Only a hero screenshot exists. Review visible message/offer issues and mark below-fold, mobile and checkout unobserved.
2. A promotion changed mid-period. Segment the baseline or label comparison confounded instead of attributing the change to the hero.
