# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic pallet usable base 120×100 cm, allowed cargo height 100 cm, cartons 40×25×20 cm at 5 kg, pallet tare 20 kg, payload including pallet 250 kg, no overhang. Layer 3×4=12 cartons; height allows 5 layers=60 cartons, but weight allows floor((250−20)/5)=46. Three full layers 36 plus 10 supported cartons could carry 46 only after stack/support validation; a simple full-layer plan is **36 cartons**, weight 200 kg, cargo height 60 cm. Four full layers 48 would weigh 260 kg and fail. No “60-carton capacity” promise.

## Boundary case 1

Trailer cube is sufficient but a pallet exceeds door height. Reject the proposed loading path until equipment or packaging changes are verified.

## Boundary case 2

Carton orientation with most units is marked “keep upright” incompatible. Exclude that rotation even if its arithmetic utilization is higher.
