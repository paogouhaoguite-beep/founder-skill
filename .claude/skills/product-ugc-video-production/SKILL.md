# Product UGC Video Production

Create a creator-style product video with a full script, produced scenes and verified render. Use an authorized real presenter or a clearly fictional demonstration character. A fabricated first-person review is not a valid shortcut to UGC.

## Choose the truthful narrator

Collect source product facts, approved assets, target audience/placement, language, desired length and the current production budget/tools. Separate three modes: a real customer's supplied experience, a contracted presenter reading brand copy, and a synthetic fictional presenter. This skill defaults to brand-script demonstration, not a claimed customer experience. Do not attribute usage history, purchases, measured results or personal feelings to an invented customer.

Use a real person's likeness or cloned voice only within their explicit permission. A licensed stock voice does not imply consent to imitate a named person. For a synthetic presenter, describe an original character and identify the portrayal as a brand demonstration; verify applicable platform disclosure requirements when publication is requested.

## Produce

Write the entire spoken/on-screen script before rendering. Use a specific factual hook, demonstrate only supported features and finish with one accurate next action. Casual delivery, handheld framing and quick cuts may shape the style, but fake order screens, review stars, “I used this for months” or unverified transformations may not supply credibility.

Read [production execution](references/production.md). Check presenter consistency, speech/lip-sync availability and product-reference support in the actual tools. Generate authorized presenter segments and keep difficult product handling in verified source footage or a separate product insert. Await each task, save its output and compose the timeline. Use existing authorization and quantity/budget; do not repeatedly ask before the requested creation steps.

Review for voice/script agreement, facial/hand artifacts, honest product contact, label stability and audible pronunciation. A presenter who appears to hold the item is not acceptable if the item visibly changes. Replace that shot with a factual product cutaway instead of concealing the defect.

## Deliver

Return actual video, script, shot provenance, presenter/voice mode and permissions, disclosure copy where applicable, captions and acceptance status. Without connected production tools, give a complete generation/edit handoff marked `not generated`; do not claim a customer or testimonial exists.
