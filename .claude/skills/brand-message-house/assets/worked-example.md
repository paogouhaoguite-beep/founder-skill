All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A 500 ml bottle fits a 68 mm bike cage, weighs 120 g and is hand-wash only. Buyers complain their wide bottles do not fit. Expected chain: 68 mm body → fits the tested cage → carry water on that ride. Pillar “easy maintenance” is rejected because no cleaning advantage is supplied. Promise: “A 500 ml bottle sized for the tested 68 mm cage.” Do not claim “fits every bike” or “lightest bottle”.
