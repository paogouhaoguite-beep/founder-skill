# Brand Impersonation Evidence

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a brand impersonation evidence packet with dated comparisons, provider attribution limits and reviewable reporting drafts.

## Merchant inputs

Official owned domains/accounts; suspected impersonation URLs; dated screenshots; customer reports; ownership/rights evidence; requested response scope.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Preserve full suspected URLs, retrieval times and screenshots alongside official comparators. Distinguish lookalike naming from observed deception.

2. Record specific copied assets, misleading identity statements or fraudulent payment representations without interacting with suspicious checkout flows.

3. Separate registrar, DNS/CDN and hosting evidence; a visible intermediary is not necessarily the origin host. Keep uncertain attribution explicit.

4. Build an incident packet with customer impact evidence stripped of personal/payment data and a factual chronology.

5. Identify the appropriate current platform/provider reporting route and the evidence it requires. Do not assert copyright/trademark infringement or filing entitlement beyond supplied rights evidence.

6. Draft a report for the authorized owner’s review and track submissions/readbacks separately. Do not file legal notices, contact operators or promise removal without explicit scope.

## Deliverable

A brand impersonation evidence packet with dated comparisons, provider attribution limits and reviewable reporting drafts.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
