## Specific changes

A brand impersonation evidence packet with dated comparisons, provider attribution limits and reviewable reporting drafts.

- Preserve full suspected URLs, retrieval times and screenshots alongside official comparators. Distinguish lookalike naming from observed deception.
- Record specific copied assets, misleading identity statements or fraudulent payment representations without interacting with suspicious checkout flows.
- Separate registrar, DNS/CDN and hosting evidence; a visible intermediary is not necessarily the origin host. Keep uncertain attribution explicit.
- Build an incident packet with customer impact evidence stripped of personal/payment data and a factual chronology.
- Identify the appropriate current platform/provider reporting route and the evidence it requires. Do not assert copyright/trademark infringement or filing entitlement beyond supplied rights evidence.
- Draft a report for the authorized owner’s review and track submissions/readbacks separately. Do not file legal notices, contact operators or promise removal without explicit scope.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions.
