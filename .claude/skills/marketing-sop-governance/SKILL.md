# Marketing SOP Governance

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a marketing SOP inventory and maintenance plan with owners, observable completion checks, change history and stop conditions.

## Merchant inputs

Existing campaign workflows; artifact locations; owners; revision history; success checks; dependencies; recurring schedule and approval scope.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Inventory repeatable marketing tasks and find the current authoritative procedure for each. Separate one-off campaign notes from a maintained SOP.

2. Give every procedure an owner, applicability, inputs, last-reviewed date and change history; stale tooling references become explicit repair items.

3. Rewrite vague completion steps into observable checks such as a rendered destination, reconciled count or provider receipt. Drafting and publishing remain distinct states.

4. Document rollback/stop conditions and handoffs for external actions. A recurring schedule does not grant future permission for every spend or message.

5. Tie each improvement to a specific observed campaign outcome and source artifact. Treat lessons as contextual evidence, not universal algorithm rules.

6. Produce a maintenance queue and review cadence appropriate to change risk. Update only procedures within scope and keep superseded versions available for incident reconstruction.

## Deliverable

A marketing SOP inventory and maintenance plan with owners, observable completion checks, change history and stop conditions.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
