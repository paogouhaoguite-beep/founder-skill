## Specific changes

A marketing SOP inventory and maintenance plan with owners, observable completion checks, change history and stop conditions.

- Inventory repeatable marketing tasks and find the current authoritative procedure for each. Separate one-off campaign notes from a maintained SOP.
- Give every procedure an owner, applicability, inputs, last-reviewed date and change history; stale tooling references become explicit repair items.
- Rewrite vague completion steps into observable checks such as a rendered destination, reconciled count or provider receipt. Drafting and publishing remain distinct states.
- Document rollback/stop conditions and handoffs for external actions. A recurring schedule does not grant future permission for every spend or message.
- Tie each improvement to a specific observed campaign outcome and source artifact. Treat lessons as contextual evidence, not universal algorithm rules.
- Produce a maintenance queue and review cadence appropriate to change risk. Update only procedures within scope and keep superseded versions available for incident reconstruction.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
