# Merchant Sonic Identity Brief

## Inputs
Collect brand attributes, audiences, existing audio, use cases, playback environments, music/voice rights and production budget. A written brief plus listening rubric is sufficient without composition tools; do not pretend to create licensed original music from a text description.

## Sound system
Map brand traits to testable acoustic choices: instrumentation/timbre, rhythm, melodic contour, density and energy. Avoid claiming a particular frequency or tempo universally signals a brand emotion. Define a short recognizable motif and permitted variations for intro, outro, product demonstration or notification where appropriate.

Specify required lengths, mono/stereo behavior, small-speaker intelligibility, start/end handling and files/stems. Distinguish a musical identity from accessibility or urgent alert sounds; product safety signals must not be replaced with decorative branding.

Evaluate candidate audio in context, not only on headphones. Test recognition and fit with the intended audience using a neutral protocol; do not fabricate scores or guarantee recall. Verify composition, sample, performer and distribution rights before use.

## Deliver
Return composer-ready brief, use-case matrix, review criteria, rights checklist and approved-versus-proposed status. Purchasing, commissioning, trademark conclusions and publication need separate scope.
