## Specific changes

A task-relative content evidence audit with information-unit comparison, unsupported claims and specific editorial improvements.

- Define the exact shopper task and comparator cohort; do not compare a transactional category page with unrelated long essays merely because a keyword overlaps.
- Create an information-unit matrix: specifications, compatibility, use conditions, comparisons, evidence, limitations and next actions. Mark whether each unit is present, correct, sourced and useful.
- Trace important claims to accessible primary evidence and the correct product/version. Distinguish hands-on experience from paraphrased manufacturer copy.
- Check clarity, authorship, transparency and completeness for the task. Do not turn E-E-A-T into a purported Google score or demand arbitrary word counts.
- Identify useful information gains over comparators, such as tested fit or a disclosed calculation. More words and copied headings do not constitute additional evidence.
- Deliver a prioritized edit plan with exact passages and missing evidence. Keep editorial adequacy separate from ranking, which cannot be inferred from this audit.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
