# Search Content Evidence Audit

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a task-relative content evidence audit with information-unit comparison, unsupported claims and specific editorial improvements.

## Merchant inputs

Target page; buyer/query task; dated comparator pages; original product evidence; author/editor expertise; claim sources; user and search observations.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Define the exact shopper task and comparator cohort; do not compare a transactional category page with unrelated long essays merely because a keyword overlaps.

2. Create an information-unit matrix: specifications, compatibility, use conditions, comparisons, evidence, limitations and next actions. Mark whether each unit is present, correct, sourced and useful.

3. Trace important claims to accessible primary evidence and the correct product/version. Distinguish hands-on experience from paraphrased manufacturer copy.

4. Check clarity, authorship, transparency and completeness for the task. Do not turn E-E-A-T into a purported Google score or demand arbitrary word counts.

5. Identify useful information gains over comparators, such as tested fit or a disclosed calculation. More words and copied headings do not constitute additional evidence.

6. Deliver a prioritized edit plan with exact passages and missing evidence. Keep editorial adequacy separate from ranking, which cannot be inferred from this audit.

## Deliverable

A task-relative content evidence audit with information-unit comparison, unsupported claims and specific editorial improvements.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
