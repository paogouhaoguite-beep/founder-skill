# Reporting, recheck and appeal contract

## Evidence status is separate from priority
- Confirmed issue: evidence establishes a material contradiction, broken function or conflict with an explicit policy requirement. This is our evidenced finding, not a Google decision.
- Risk signal: suspicious or potentially misleading but insufficiently established; prescribe verification before definitive remediation.
- Merchant verification needed: account access, business records or fulfillment evidence required.
- No issue found: applies only to inspected scope.
- Check limited: access/tool/data constraints prevented a conclusion.

Priorities describe remediation impact, not likelihood of suspension:
- P0: major evidenced harm such as incorrect charging, clearly false identity or a fundamental inability to purchase as offered.
- P1: significant purchase-decision contradiction or missing material terms.
- P2: clarity, discoverability or maintenance improvement.
Do not infer P0 from a temporary fetch failure. Do not invent compliance scores or approval probabilities.

## Complete report
Use the user's requested/conversation language, independent of storefront language. Save a readable Markdown or HTML file when file tools are available; otherwise deliver the full report in chat. A file-format failure must not block substantive delivery. Include:
1. Leading conclusion: key actionable issues, what is and is not established, and unresolved evidence needed before preparing review.
2. Scope: domain, time, countries/languages/currencies, source/access, inspected pages, sample count/rationale and limitations.
3. Findings: ID, evidence status, priority, exact URL/source, timestamp, market/variant/quantity/price conditions, actual observation, comparison or policy basis, specific consumer impact, uncertainty, proposed fix and verification procedure. Link actual supporting evidence, not generic policies alone.
4. Merchant verification checklist: only unresolved checks, minimal evidence needed and its purpose. Ask for unrelated sensitive fields to be redacted.
5. Remediation order: dependencies, direct fixes, facts the merchant must confirm, and affected pages/data to recheck.
6. Sources: relevant official policy URLs and access dates, plus actual evidence links.
A concise conclusion is not a substitute for the full findings and scope. Do not fill missing evidence with plausible details.

## Recheck
Reuse prior issue IDs and matching pages/items/markets. Read fresh evidence for each item and record fixed / partly fixed / not fixed / unverifiable, before/after snippets and time. Verify that fixes did not introduce conflicting promises. Successful writes or merchant assertions alone do not establish a fix. Website remediation and GMC status recovery are separate outcomes. Follow any existing authorized scope for edits; otherwise deliver reviewable edits/proposals without writing externally.

## Appeal draft only when requested
Include actual business situation, confirmed issues and causes (or explicitly unresolved causes), completed and verified changes, supporting documents/links, and pending verification. A user disputing a finding need not falsely admit it. Never claim unverified remediation or manufacture credentials, reviews or proof. No automatic appeal submission or request-review action without explicit authorization; existing authorization suffices. If submission was separately authorized and supported, read back its receipt/status and do not equate submission with approval. Do not retry an uncertain submission until its state is checked.
