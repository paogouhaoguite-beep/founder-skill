# Official policy baseline and provenance

At each audit, read the current official pages relevant to the actual finding and record access date. This reference is a starting map, not a frozen legal checklist or a claim of Google certification.

- [Misrepresentation](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127): inaccurate business/product representation, misleading claims, omitted material information and unavailable offers. Google can consider website, account and third-party evidence.
- [Product data specification](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112): follow current links for price, currency, availability, sale terms and applicable product attributes.
- Discover current official shipping, returns, website and account-review instructions through Merchant Center Help when those issues affect this audit. Do not invent policy URLs or treat third-party checklists as binding rules.

For each policy-based finding distinguish an explicit requirement from a Google recommendation and our usability advice. Quote only the short supporting passage needed and link its source; do not copy entire policies. A missing field or page becomes a finding only after its applicability and purchase impact are established. If official access fails, disclose that freshness could not be verified and withhold definitive conclusions dependent on changing requirements. Do not prescribe legal return windows or jurisdiction-specific obligations without current authoritative support.

Design provenance: ShopChief's own audit workflow, informed by the public entrypoint of [shopify-gmc-misrepresentation-auditor](https://github.com/lvsao/shopify-skill-hub/tree/main/skills/shopify-gmc-misrepresentation-auditor) and Google's policy. No upstream audit scripts are bundled or executed. This skill does not simulate Google's internal review or guarantee reinstatement.
