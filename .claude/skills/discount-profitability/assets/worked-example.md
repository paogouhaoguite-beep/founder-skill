# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic price USD 100, fixed per-order variable cost 60, no percentage fee. Base contribution 40. A 20% discount leaves revenue 80 and contribution 20, requiring 40/20 = 2× orders to preserve total contribution. Two successive 10% discounts instead leave 81 and contribution 21, requiring approximately 1.905×. These are break-even requirements, not predicted demand.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Reproduce the 2× and 1.905× requirements without adding percentages arithmetically.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If contribution becomes negative after a shipping promotion, flag loss per order and avoid recommending more volume as a remedy.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
