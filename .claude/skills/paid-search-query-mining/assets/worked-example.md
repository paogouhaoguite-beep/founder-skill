# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic campaign total: 200 clicks and USD 300. Disclosed queries: 120 clicks and USD 180, giving 60% click and spend coverage; 80 clicks remain undisclosed. “washable travel pouch” has 20 mature clicks, cost USD 30 and zero purchases. CPC = 1.50. At an allowed CPA of USD 30, required CVR = 5%. The fixed-sample zero-conversion upper bound is 1 − 0.05^(1/20) = 13.91%, so these data do not rule out a 5% CVR. Label uncertain; do not report its USD 30 as proven wasted.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given the report covers 120 of 200 clicks, qualify every query-mix conclusion and leave the 80-click remainder unknown.
2. Given 20 clicks and zero purchases for a relevant query, calculate the economic requirement and uncertainty rather than automatically negate it.
