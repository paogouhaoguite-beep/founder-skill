# DTC social content

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=dtc-social-content&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Read the product facts, audience, target platform, brand voice, licensed assets and objective. Use the merchant's real benefits, use cases and demonstrated results. Never turn a draft example into an invented customer story, testimonial, product test or revenue claim.

## Select a useful content angle
Choose among a product demonstration, problem/solution explanation, fit or material comparison, care tutorial, behind-the-scenes process with real evidence, or an honest customer question. Match the viewer's knowledge and the product's visual affordance. A serviceable content series is preferable to a universal daily posting quota.

For a short video, provide opening frame/hook, shot-by-shot action, spoken words, on-screen text, product-proof source, approximate pacing and CTA. For a carousel, provide a coherent slide sequence with copy and image direction. For a post, deliver actual caption variants and an asset brief, not only a list of themes.

## Preserve truth and accessibility
Claims must be supported by merchant facts or cited evidence. Disclose synthetic demonstrations where they could be mistaken for real testing. Keep material, size, color and use limitations consistent with the product. Use readable text, captions/alt guidance and realistic filming instructions. Verify current platform field/asset limits before scheduling; no claim that a fixed hashtag count or posting time guarantees reach.

## Repurpose with a purpose
Adapt the same evidence to each selected platform's format and viewer context. A tutorial can become a short video, carousel and product care excerpt without inventing three new experiences. Link to the most relevant real PDP/collection/article when the format supports it; do not force a promotion into unrelated community replies.

## Deliver and measure
Provide the final content, exact required assets, target URL/UTM suggestion, proposed timing and review checks. Save drafts; publish only to the specified account and approved content when authorized. Confirm actual post IDs/URLs after posting.

Measure qualified destination visits and product actions alongside completion/saves/comments. Engagement is not revenue; use attributable outcomes and label assisted or unknown paths.

## Example request
"Turn these verified product photos and care instructions into three Instagram Reels scripts and one carousel for a DTC wool-knit brand. Keep all claims factual and deliver drafts only."
