# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic start-of-month cohort: 200 active subscriptions; 12 voluntarily cancel; 10 distinct other subscriptions fail payment, of whom 6 recover by the defined cutoff and 4 are lost. Ten other subscriptions skip one shipment but remain active. Opening-cohort lost subscriptions = 12+4=16 and subscription loss rate = 16/200 = 8%; do not add the 10 skips to churn. A USD 40 shipment with USD 25 variable costs yields USD 15 contribution. A one-cycle USD 8 save discount leaves USD 7; two successful cycles, discounted then normal, yield USD 22 before other retention costs. An accepted offer with no successful renewal is not that USD 22 outcome.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given ten one-cycle skips, keep them separate from lost subscriptions and document the active-state definition.
2. Given a warehouse-committed order after the cancellation cutoff, distinguish future-renewal cancellation from the pending shipment and do not promise interception without evidence.
