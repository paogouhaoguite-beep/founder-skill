# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: 50 sessions from verified assistant domains, 10 from an unknown lookalike domain and 200 direct sessions. Expected confirmed AI referrals = 50; 10 remain unclassified and 200 remain direct. If 4 confirmed unique orders match the 50 sessions under the stated model, credited session-to-order rate = 8%; it is not the conversion rate of all AI-exposed shoppers.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given a source domain merely contains “chatgpt” in an unrelated hostname, do not classify by substring alone.

2. Given a customer mentions AI in a survey but the order is already credited to email, preserve both evidence types without counting two orders.
