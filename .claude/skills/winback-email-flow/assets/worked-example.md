# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic cohort has a merchant-supported 75-day repurchase review point. Randomized eligible groups: 200 contacted, 200 held out; after the same completed window, 18 and 12 repurchase. Rates are 9% and 6%, observed difference +3 percentage points; 200×0.03 = 6 estimated incremental orders, with sampling uncertainty. At USD 22 contribution/order after incentives and USD 20 total contact cost, point estimate = 6×22 − 20 = USD 112. This is not statistically established lift from those counts alone.

Draft: “Ready for another refill? Your previous blend is available in the same size. Check the ingredients and current delivery estimate before ordering. If your routine has changed, update your preferences below.” No discount or deadline is invented.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given durable luggage with sparse repurchase history, do not force a 90-day lapse rule; deliver a suitable product/newness hypothesis instead.
2. Given a repurchase after message 1, remove the customer from subsequent winback offers and maintain the experiment assignment for analysis.
