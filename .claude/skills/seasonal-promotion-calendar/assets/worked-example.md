# Synthetic seasonal plan
A merchant-defined gifting cutoff is December 12, verified from its carrier/service commitment; promotional stock arrives December 3 pending receipt. Draft landing and email by November 28, confirm stock December 3, launch only after receipt and checkout offer QA, switch messaging after December 12 to avoid promising holiday arrival. If stock slips, promote an in-stock substitute only after checking its margin and fit.

At USD 50 list price and USD 30 known variable cost, a 20% discount gives USD 40 revenue and USD 10 pre-ad contribution before any missing costs—not a 20% margin.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. Promotion inventory arrival is uncertain. Mark launch conditional and provide an in-stock fallback rather than publish a firm availability claim.
2. Two discounts stack unintentionally. Model the actual combined price and require checkout verification before activation.
