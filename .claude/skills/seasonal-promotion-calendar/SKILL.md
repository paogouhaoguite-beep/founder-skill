# Seasonal promotion calendar

> By [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=seasonal-promotion-calendar&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill) · Practical workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. No ShopChief account required.

## Start with the merchant's trading constraints
Read target market/timezone, selling season and date range, products/inventory, supplier and fulfillment lead times, carrier cutoffs, margin/cost coverage, audience permissions and previous promotion evidence. Verify current calendar dates and carrier promises from authoritative sources when available; offline use supplied dates with their verification status.

Choose occasions relevant to the buyer and assortment. Do not fill every holiday or assume northern-hemisphere seasons. Distinguish demand planning, promotion launch, last-order-for-arrival and clearance dates. A delivery cutoff is a service condition, not a reason to invent guaranteed arrival.

## Work backward from a viable offer
For each selected window define eligible products/customers, price/discount mechanism, exclusions, stacking, stock cap and actual end condition. Calculate contribution under the offered price using the merchant's variable-cost basis and known fulfillment/returns. Show unknowns. Scarcity claims must reflect real stock/deadlines; a resettable countdown is not a calendar strategy.

Schedule inventory readiness, landing/creative drafts, tracking QA, email/social preparation, launch, cutoff messaging, shutdown and post-period reconciliation with owners and dependencies. Separate returning customers from new-buyer acquisition where the offer and measurement differ. Build a fallback for delayed replenishment, early stockout and delivery disruption rather than simply extending an expired promise.

## Deliver an executable calendar
Output `local date/time | market | occasion | offer/SKUs | dependency | asset/task | owner | readiness gate | fallback`. Include briefed content requirements and budget decisions, not an invented daily content quota. Show measurement windows for orders, net revenue, contribution, returns and unsubscribes; compare against a relevant historical/holdout basis when it exists, acknowledging seasonality and pull-forward effects.

The calendar does not schedule posts, create discounts, send email or authorize spend. Execute those only within explicit user scope and read back actual created objects. Missing stock/cost/date evidence leaves that activation gate pending rather than silently estimated.
