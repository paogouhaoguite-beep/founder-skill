## Specific changes

A capacity-feasible social channel portfolio with workload arithmetic, pilot caps and keep/exit criteria.

- Map audience evidence to each candidate channel and state whether it comes from customers, analytics or a proxy research tool.
- List the channel’s required operations: publishing, replying, moderation, reporting and creative production. Verify actual access; missing API access can be handled by manual exports.
- Estimate weekly workload from units × time per unit, including ongoing moderation. Avoid choosing cadence from an industry minimum.
- Rank channels as primary, secondary or listening-only by audience fit, meaningful outcomes and feasible staffing. Explain rejected channels and opportunity cost.
- For a new channel predeclare a time-boxed pilot, spend/hour cap, decision date and outcome target. Use observed merchant economics for graduation, not a borrowed CPA.
- Return a portfolio that fits the total capacity and leaves incident headroom. A staffing plan is not permission to post, contact members or purchase media.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
