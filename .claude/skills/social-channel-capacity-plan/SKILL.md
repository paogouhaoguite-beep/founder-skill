# Social Channel Capacity Plan

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a capacity-feasible social channel portfolio with workload arithmetic, pilot caps and keep/exit criteria.

## Merchant inputs

Buyer/channel evidence; objective; staff hours; asset production and response costs; current channel results; budget and access limitations.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Map audience evidence to each candidate channel and state whether it comes from customers, analytics or a proxy research tool.

2. List the channel’s required operations: publishing, replying, moderation, reporting and creative production. Verify actual access; missing API access can be handled by manual exports.

3. Estimate weekly workload from units × time per unit, including ongoing moderation. Avoid choosing cadence from an industry minimum.

4. Rank channels as primary, secondary or listening-only by audience fit, meaningful outcomes and feasible staffing. Explain rejected channels and opportunity cost.

5. For a new channel predeclare a time-boxed pilot, spend/hour cap, decision date and outcome target. Use observed merchant economics for graduation, not a borrowed CPA.

6. Return a portfolio that fits the total capacity and leaves incident headroom. A staffing plan is not permission to post, contact members or purchase media.

## Deliverable

A capacity-feasible social channel portfolio with workload arithmetic, pilot caps and keep/exit criteria.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
