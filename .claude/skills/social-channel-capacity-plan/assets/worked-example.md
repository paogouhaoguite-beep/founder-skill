# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: 8 available hours/week. Channel A needs 3 posts × 1 hour plus 2 reply hours = 5 hours. Channel B needs 2 posts × 1 hour plus 2 reply hours = 4 hours. Both require 9 hours, exceeding capacity by 1. Choose A as primary and B as a 1-hour listening lane, leaving 2 hours for reporting/incidents. State the audience evidence behind A rather than choosing solely from the arithmetic.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given no audience evidence for a channel, mark its fit unvalidated and propose research rather than an assured launch.

2. Given moderation demand rises by 4 hours, reduce planned production or staff the deficit; do not hide response work.
