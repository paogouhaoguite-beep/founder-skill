# Merchant Voiceover Production

## Inputs
Collect approved script/product facts, target audience/locale, duration, pronunciation glossary, voice preferences and rights/consent for any voice reference. A narration script and direction sheet are complete fallback artifacts when audio tools are unavailable. Do not imitate a real person without appropriate permission or present synthetic speech as their endorsement.

## Script and direction
Align narration to visible actions using a two-column time/visual/speech table. Speak the necessary claim conditions and avoid compressing safety information to fit a target. Read numbers, units, acronyms and product names deliberately; provide pronunciation notes without changing facts.

Choose delivery traits such as pace, energy and pauses from the purpose, not an unverifiable “best converting” voice. For localized narration, adapt phrasing and pronunciation with native review while preserving commercial and safety meaning. Timing may differ by language; do not force the translated speech into the original duration by making it unintelligible.

If generation/recording is authorized and available, produce takes with documented settings. Listen to the entire output, verify every word against the approved script, inspect clipping/noise and compare actual duration to visual timing. Re-record material errors rather than conceal them under music.

## Deliver
Return script, pronunciation/direction sheet, actual audio if produced, timing and QA results. Keep voice provenance and consent in internal records. Publishing or using a person’s likeness requires its own scope.
