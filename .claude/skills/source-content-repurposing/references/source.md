## Specific changes

A source-grounded repurposing pack containing finished native copy, source-to-output traceability and a reviewable distribution queue.

- Extract a source ledger of claims, stories, quotations, demonstrations and useful instructions, preserving passage or timestamp IDs.
- Choose atoms that stand alone and answer a real shopper question. Separate a source observation from an inference; do not invent personal experience to make a stronger hook.
- Map each atom to a suitable output such as caption, carousel, short script or email excerpt. Preserve sufficient context for readers who never saw the source.
- Draft actual variants with truthful hooks and a completed payoff. A curiosity gap must close; a numerical headline requires the matching sourced number.
- Check each output against product facts, quote/asset permissions, offer freshness and brand tone. Do not turn a source warning into an endorsement by cropping its context.
- Return finished copy plus a source-to-output matrix, asset needs and a feasible distribution queue. Track draft/review/published states and actual receipts separately.
- For each short-video hook provide spoken words, the opening visual frame and the later source-backed payoff, including a muted-viewing option. For a caption paired with media, complement rather than merely repeat the visible scene. Produce contrasting hook hypotheses without calling fixed opening seconds an algorithm law.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
