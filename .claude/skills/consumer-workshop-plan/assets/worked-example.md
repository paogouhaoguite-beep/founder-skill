# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A 20-minute coffee workshop: 2 minutes welcome, 10 grind-size demo, 5 questions, 3 care/next step. Draft invite: “Join a 20-minute demonstration of adjusting your grinder. We will show the supplied model and leave time for questions.” Of 100 registrants, 60 attend and 12 click the guide: attendance 60%, attendee guide-click rate 20%. Do not report 12 purchases or assume a commercial lift.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given recording permission is absent, plan notes/captions that do not require recording participants.

2. Given a demo uses a different model, disclose that limitation and do not claim settings apply to every product.
