# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic merchant needs 500 refill pouches monthly,250ml, leak test protocol Q2, first receipt day 45. Food-contact suitability is mandatory with applicable test evidence; printed color match is preferred. Manufacturer A states 250ml capability but has no test report: qualify pending, not approved. Reseller B can deliver in 20 days but manufacturer identity unknown: traceability gap.

RFI questions: confirm exact pouch construction/test scope; quote 500/1000 units with setup/tooling; identify factory and receipt lead time; provide sample acceptance timeline. No RFQ sent.

## Boundary case 1

Requester says “eco-friendly packaging” with no material/use requirement. Record ambiguous objective and request evidence-bound criteria; do not invent certification.

## Boundary case 2

A supplier website names a product category but no actual size or production evidence. Retain as unqualified lead, not a capable shortlisted supplier.
