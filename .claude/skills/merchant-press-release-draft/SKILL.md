# Merchant Press Release Draft

Use for a real product launch, store opening, retail partnership or published business milestone. Inputs: verified change, product/partner names, release date and timezone, location, price/availability, source documents, quote permissions, factual boilerplate and monitored media contact. Work from supplied evidence locally; external verification may be used when available, but never substitute assumptions for missing facts.

Isolate what changed and why the intended reader cares. If no actual news change is supported, explain that a product update or editorial article may be more suitable; do not invent a first, largest or breakthrough claim. Separate planned, announced and available dates.

Build a facts/approvals ledger before prose. Write an active headline and a standalone lead containing who, what, when and practical significance. Apply inverted-pyramid order: essential news, supporting mechanics, approved perspective, context, availability, boilerplate/contact. Specific distributor limits govern length; there is no universal character count or exactly-two-quotes requirement.

Use quotations only as exact approved text. A requested suggested quote must be marked proposed, awaiting named speaker approval, and excluded from a publication-ready version until cleared. A quote cannot launder an unsupported factual claim. Prices require currency and relevant scope; use current approved market availability, not assumed global launch.

State FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE or the precise authorized embargo including timezone. An embargo label alone does not bind recipients. Draft a factual boilerplate without fabricated traction and verify the actual contact. Deliver the complete release, evidence/approval ledger and distribution-status note. Do not send to journalists, submit a wire, upload or publish unless explicitly authorized; a completed draft is not media coverage.
