# Merchant Social Carousel Production

## Inputs
Require audience task, factual source, channel/current format constraints, brand voice, assets and intended action. A slide-by-slide script is sufficient without graphics tools. Do not turn a carousel draft into a posting action.

## Sequence
Select one useful takeaway and define the first-slide promise. Outline a progression that earns the conclusion: context/problem, explanation or steps, concrete example, limitation and relevant next action. Use as many panels as the content needs within the destination constraints, not a universal fixed count.

Each slide gets a headline, concise body, visual role and source/claim note. Keep continuity of terms and product variants. Avoid arbitrary cliffhangers, unsupported numbers and pseudo-scientific authority. The cover must match what the remaining slides actually teach.

Design readable hierarchy at phone size with consistent alignment, safe margins and contrast. Check that each slide still makes sense when shared separately, retaining critical qualifiers. If illustrations are generated, verify text and product fidelity. Provide accessible captions/alt text where the destination supports them.

## Deliver
Return complete slide copy, layout/asset brief, source checks, caption draft and render/publication status. Do not invent performance claims or auto-publish.
