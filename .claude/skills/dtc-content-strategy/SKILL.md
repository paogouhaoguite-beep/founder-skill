# DTC content strategy

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=dtc-content-strategy&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Use the actual catalog, market, buyer questions, support themes, existing content, brand claims and production capacity. Keep questions from customers separate from keyword-volume estimates. A target topic without search data is a hypothesis, not measured demand.

## Map content to a merchant decision
Group opportunities by buyer job: choose the right product, compare alternatives, understand fit/material, solve a usage problem, care for the purchase, or replenish/replace it. Identify a useful answer and the natural product/collection handoff for each. Avoid producing many nearly identical pages for locations or keyword variants with no distinct buyer value.

Inventory existing URLs before proposing new ones. Choose create/update/merge/skip based on intent overlap and evidence; a different keyword spelling does not necessarily need another article. Public competitor pages can reveal formats and gaps, but cannot establish their conversion or revenue.

## Build the plan
For each selected piece provide audience/question, intent, unique merchant evidence, outline, required product facts/images, destination URL, suggested internal links, author/reviewer, distribution route, effort and measurement. Prioritize high buyer relevance and feasible evidence before raw volume. Keep unsupported claims and missing assets visible.

Plan at the merchant's sustainable cadence. Separate searchable guides from shareable demonstrations and design a distribution action for each: opted-in email segment, relevant community contribution, creator demonstration or a native social format. A plan is not authorization to post or contact anyone.

## Handoff and review
Deliver a calendar plus complete briefs for the requested scope. A writing request should continue into finished articles with available blog-article-writer guidance rather than stop at a calendar. Use a real relevant CTA after answering the reader; do not turn every paragraph into promotion.

Measure qualified visits, product/collection visits, assisted paths and repeat engagement with clear attribution limits. Rankings or impressions alone do not prove orders. Use dated first-party results to decide update, expand or retire.

## Example request
"Plan six useful guides for a linen bedding store selling in the UK. We have material specs and care photos but no keyword subscription. Identify evidence gaps and link each topic to the right collection."
