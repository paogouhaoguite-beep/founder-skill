# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic week A net revenue USD 10,000, variable non-ad costs 6,000, ads 2,000: post-ad contribution 2,000. Week B revenue 12,000, costs 8,000, ads 2,500: contribution 1,500. Revenue rises 20%, contribution falls 25%. If receipts for B are 9,000, do not replace revenue with receipts or infer the 3,000 difference is fraud; reconcile timing and payment status.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Reproduce +20% revenue and -25% contribution and prioritize investigating cost/mix and spend rather than declaring a healthy week.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

When current week has only four complete days, compare matched days or mark the weekly change unavailable; do not extrapolate an exact seven-day result as fact.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
