## Specific changes

A packaging and unboxing marketing brief with concrete panel/insert copy, cost tradeoffs and prototype checks.

- Map the customer’s sequence from parcel identification through opening, product discovery, care and disposal. Separate protective/mandatory information from promotional surfaces.
- Define a packaging hierarchy: product/variant identification, safe opening/care, required notices and a useful next step. Do not crowd functional labels with brand storytelling.
- Specify visual and copy direction on a supplied dieline or surface inventory; do not invent structural safety, material performance or printer tolerances.
- Compare marketing additions against quoted unit cost, packing time, shipment dimensions and waste. Use sourced local disposal instructions rather than generic “eco-friendly” promises.
- Draft concrete insert or panel copy with accurate care and optional support/guide links. Keep review requests neutral and separate from rewards or suppression of criticism.
- Return the brief, copy, proof requirements and prototype inspection plan; approval of artwork does not certify shipping protection, legal labels or manufacturing quality.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
