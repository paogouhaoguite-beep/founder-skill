# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: A tea tin uses a protective mailer; an optional card costs $0.12 and adds 8 seconds packing time. At 1,000 orders, card cost is $120 and extra packing time 8,000 seconds = 2.22 hours. Insert draft: “Keep your tea sealed and away from moisture. Scan the guide for brewing notes. Need help with your order? Contact the support address on your receipt.” No “100% sustainable” claim is added without material/disposal evidence.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given required safety text conflicts with the visual layout, preserve legibility and escalate design rather than shrink the label invisibly.

2. Given recyclability depends on local facilities or mixed materials, state the verified limitations instead of a universal recycling promise.
