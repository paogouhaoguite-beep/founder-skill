# Packaging Marketing Brief

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a packaging and unboxing marketing brief with concrete panel/insert copy, cost tradeoffs and prototype checks.

## Merchant inputs

Product dimensions/fragility; actual packaging constraints; brand voice; required labels; unboxing journey; cost quotes; shipping context; reuse/recycling evidence.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Map the customer’s sequence from parcel identification through opening, product discovery, care and disposal. Separate protective/mandatory information from promotional surfaces.

2. Define a packaging hierarchy: product/variant identification, safe opening/care, required notices and a useful next step. Do not crowd functional labels with brand storytelling.

3. Specify visual and copy direction on a supplied dieline or surface inventory; do not invent structural safety, material performance or printer tolerances.

4. Compare marketing additions against quoted unit cost, packing time, shipment dimensions and waste. Use sourced local disposal instructions rather than generic “eco-friendly” promises.

5. Draft concrete insert or panel copy with accurate care and optional support/guide links. Keep review requests neutral and separate from rewards or suppression of criticism.

6. Return the brief, copy, proof requirements and prototype inspection plan; approval of artwork does not certify shipping protection, legal labels or manufacturing quality.

## Deliverable

A packaging and unboxing marketing brief with concrete panel/insert copy, cost tradeoffs and prototype checks.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
