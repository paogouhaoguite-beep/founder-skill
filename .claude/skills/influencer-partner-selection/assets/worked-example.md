# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic creator A: USD 350 total cost, median 10,000 comparable views, supplied 60% target-market share. A rough geographic-view proxy is 6,000 and cost per 1,000 proxy views is 350/6,000×1,000 = USD 58.33. Creator B: USD 300, median 15,000 views but unknown geography. B's gross CPM proxy is USD 20, but it is not comparable to A's qualified proxy. Neither proxy is unique reach, guaranteed exposure or expected sales.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given B lacks audience geography, mark it unknown and avoid fabricating a qualified-view count to force a ranking.
2. Given a candidate has huge giveaway views and low ordinary views, calculate the declared comparable sample separately and explain exclusions.
