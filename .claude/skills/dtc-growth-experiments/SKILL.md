# DTC growth experiments

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=dtc-growth-experiments&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Read current traffic sources, catalog, market, product margin, inventory, budget, team capacity and measurement quality. First identify the bottleneck: discovery, qualified visits, product understanding, checkout, repeat purchase or fulfillment. Do not prescribe more acquisition when the store cannot fulfill profitably.

## Build candidates matched to the product
Consider buyer-question guides, product demonstrations, relevant creator sampling, high-intent search, a useful sizing/selection tool, a compatible product bundle, consented replenishment reminders, a customer referral offer or an in-person niche partnership. Select by the merchant's audience and product; app-store launches, SaaS lifetime deals and developer outreach are not defaults for a physical-goods brand.

For each candidate specify the audience, observed reason it might work, offer/content, acquisition route, actual destination, prerequisites, owner, effort, spending ceiling, measurement and stop condition. Include operational and contribution costs such as samples, discount, shipping, returns and creative production; ad spend is only one cost.

## Prioritize transparently
Compare likely reach evidence, buyer relevance, controllability, effort, time to learn and downside. If using a score, expose its inputs and uncertainty; an ordinal priority is not a forecast. Prefer a small executable set over a long list when the user has limited capacity. Preserve an explicitly requested broader scope.

Produce an execution-ready brief for the chosen experiment: final content/destination requirements, source tracking, target group, observation window, decision threshold and required permissions. No unsolicited outreach, account creation, publishing, ads or recurring automation.

## Learn without overstating
Keep impressions, visitors, first orders, returning customers and contribution separate. Interpret small samples as uncertain. Compare with an appropriate baseline and account for promotions/seasonality; a channel's attributed sales are not automatically incremental. Record what to repeat, revise or stop and why.

## Example request
"We sell reusable lunch bags in Canada, have CAD 500 and five hours per week, and know our contribution per order. Pick three growth experiments we can actually run and show the cost and learning plan."
