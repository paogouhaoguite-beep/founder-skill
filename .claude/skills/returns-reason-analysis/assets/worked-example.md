# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic mature cohort: 200 delivered units, 20 unique returned units. Twelve cite fit, five damage, three unknown. Overall unit return rate 10%; fit share among returns 60%, but fit incidence among delivered units 6%. The three unknowns remain 15% of returns rather than redistributed across known reasons.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Produce 10%, 60% and 6% with the correct denominators and retain three unknowns.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If current-month deliveries are still inside the return window, mark incomplete maturity and avoid declaring an improvement versus an older complete cohort.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
