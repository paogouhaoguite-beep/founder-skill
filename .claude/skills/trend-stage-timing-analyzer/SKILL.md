# Product timing and trend evidence

> From [ShopChief](https://shopchief.ai/?utm_source=trend-stage-timing-analyzer&utm_medium=agent_skill&utm_campaign=commerce_skills&utm_content=skill_header) · AI workflows for independent ecommerce and DTC sellers. Works independently; no ShopChief account required.

Judge timing for the merchant's actual product/category and target market. Do not default a missing category to home decor. Reuse the product brief and existing research; ask only if the object cannot be identified. Read [DataForSEO contract](references/dataforseo-contract.md) before querying.

## Evidence
Use matching keyword monthly history and available multi-year normalized trends to separate seasonal repetition, durable demand and new growth. Do not add normalized trend indices across keywords or equate them with search counts. Check representative current SERPs and visible marketplace products when they explain competitive structure. Public ad libraries may provide qualitative creative/advertiser examples; incomplete indexed results cannot establish market-wide counts, saturation or ROI. A long-running ad is a persistence signal, never proof of profit.

Compare time windows, geography, synonyms and brand vs generic terms. A mature stable category may still be attractive with differentiation; flat search demand is not automatically saturation or No-go. One snapshot of review count or ranking cannot establish sales velocity. Require dated comparable observations for trend claims.

## Decision
Report observed demand shape, seasonality, competition, evidence limitations and a Go/Test/No-go recommendation with reasons. Missing/conflicting signals lower confidence and can yield Test; do not force a 2-of-3 vote from incomparable evidence or expand paid research without the contract's scope/budget rules.

Assess feasibility using actual pre-ad contribution, acquisition scenarios, inventory lead time, differentiation and the merchant's budget. There is no universal 50% gross-margin, 30%-ad-spend or ad-count threshold. Without costs/conversion data, timing may be discussed but profitability remains undetermined.

## Deliverable
A concise decision brief or, when a full report is requested, [report template](templates/product-research-report.md): target/period, evidence by source, stage as a qualified interpretation, feasible test, stop condition, and next verification date. Save dated evidence for later comparison. Monitoring only starts when requested and actually scheduled through available tools. Pass existing evidence to launch/content work if requested; do not initiate sourcing or execution outside scope.
