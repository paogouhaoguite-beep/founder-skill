# Timing evidence and decision report

Use the current merchant's product, geography and customer language. Cite comparable dated observations and distinguish observed data, estimates and interpretation.

Report: decision and confidence; product/market/time window; demand and seasonality; competitive/advertising examples and sampling limitations; pre-ad unit economics and missing costs; disconfirming evidence; bounded test and stop condition; next verification baseline/date; reusable evidence references and known provider costs.

Public ad examples do not establish complete advertiser counts or ROI. Product rankings, review counts and supplier listings are snapshots, not sales velocity; historical claims need comparable dated evidence. Stable mature demand does not imply no opportunity. Thresholds require category-specific evidence and merchant economics. Do not invent platform refresh delays or universal saturation cutoffs.

Reuse existing DataForSEO history/SERP evidence. Query additional sources only when they change the decision within the declared scope and query contract. Missing inputs remain explicitly unknown; a full report does not require fictitious numbers or a rigid section count.
