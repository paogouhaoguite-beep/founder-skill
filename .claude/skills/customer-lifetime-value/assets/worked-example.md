# Synthetic 90-day value
Two mature acquired customers: A contributes USD 30 on first order and USD 20 on a repeat; B contributes USD 25 and never repeats. Observed pre-acquisition contribution/customer=(30+20+25)/2=USD 37.50. Dividing by the one repeater gives USD 75 and is wrong. At acquisition spend USD 60 for this cohort, CAC=USD 30; cumulative contribution covers it by day 90, though the exact payback day requires dated contributions.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. A newer cohort has only 20 days of history. Exclude it from a mature 90-day average or present it separately with censoring.
2. Fulfillment cost is missing. Report revenue and partial cost coverage; do not call the partial subtotal net profit or fully observed LTV.
