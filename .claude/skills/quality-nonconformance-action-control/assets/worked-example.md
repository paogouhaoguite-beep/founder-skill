# Synthetic worked example

Synthetic cap spec diameter 10.0±0.2mm; measured 10.4 on 3 of 20 sampled units in lotL of 500. Finding 3/20 sample nonconforming; **do not infer all 500 defective**. Lot held by quality owner pending expanded trace. Tool wear is a hypothesis requiring measurement. Proposed fixture update installed day 2 proves implementation; a planned follow-up sample under approved protocol is still pending, so CAPA stays open.

## Boundary case 1

Supplier asks use-as-is to meet launch. Missing quality/engineering approval: keep disposition pending; delivery urgency cannot approve a deviation.

## Boundary case 2

Instrument calibration expired. Record measurement-validity gap and request approved remeasurement; do not erase observed concern or pronounce the lot conforming.
