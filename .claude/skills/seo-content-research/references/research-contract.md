# SEO evidence contract

## Required inputs

- Existing product and confirmed selling price/currency.
- Target market and customer language.
- Current channel and the business signal motivating research, such as rising CPC.
- Existing pages or content when known.

Do not infer that the product is a new opportunity merely because demand evidence is requested. Keep facts, external evidence, calculations, and assumptions separate.

## Evidence collection

Evidence is collected directly in this skill with documented API requests through `dataforseo_api_request` and public web research — no separate evidence delegation. Choose only the research routes that change the content direction (see the routing section in SKILL.md), follow the budget caps in `references/dataforseo-contract.md`, and stop when the plan is supportable.

Collect only evidence that changes content direction: search intent, topic language, SERP/content formats, audience questions, commercial versus informational boundaries, and current constraints.

Given keyword lists that need metric values (volume, KD, CPC, competition, intent) are looked up with the `keyword-analysis` skill; do not re-query those metrics here.
