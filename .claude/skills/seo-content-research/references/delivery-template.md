# SEO content direction

Lead with the recommended content direction and why it fits the existing product.

## Priority plan

For each priority include: intent cluster, customer question, recommended page/content format, angle, funnel role, evidence, confidence, and the next measurable check.

## Page mapping

Map each cluster to an existing page, a proposed new page, or a deliberate exclusion. Avoid keyword cannibalization and distinguish product-page copy from editorial content.

## Briefs

Give a compact title direction, primary intent, audience problem, proof needed, outline, internal-link destination, CTA role, and unsafe/unverified claims to avoid.

## Boundaries

List assumptions, risks/blockers, checks completed, unchecked items, and the recommended next action. Do not include a product opportunity verdict.

## Requested finished content
If the request includes writing, include the finished article/page/copy using verified product facts and the same evidence. Save evidence references, target URL, complete copy and next measurement baseline. A planning-only request does not authorize publication.
