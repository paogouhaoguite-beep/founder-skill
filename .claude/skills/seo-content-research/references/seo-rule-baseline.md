# Verified SEO rule baseline / SEO 规则依据

Reviewed 2026-09-12 against primary Google documentation. These are editorial and evidence rules, not a fabricated ranking score. Recheck current platform-specific requirements before deployment.

- Titles should identify the page clearly and concisely. There is no fixed Google title character maximum; display truncation depends on device width. Title/H1 may legitimately match; judge accuracy, hierarchy and clarity rather than literal duplication. [Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).
- Descriptive anchor text depends on source context and the destination. Different natural anchors can point to the same page. Do not enforce one repeated anchor or a fixed link quota. [Links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
- No minimum word count or mandatory FAQ count earns a ranking. Deliver sufficient, original, useful facts and answers. [SEO guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).
- FAQ content may help real customers, but Google stopped FAQ rich results in May 2026; do not sell FAQPage as a Google rich-result/GEO requirement. Schema.org validity and an engine's rich-result support are different. [Google update log](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- Variant canonical decisions depend on actual URL architecture and distinct content. Parameter-selected variants generally consolidate to the principal URL; independent substantive variant pages require a consistent verified strategy. Keep links, sitemap, canonical and product variant data aligned. [Ecommerce URLs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/designing-a-url-structure-for-ecommerce-sites), [canonical consolidation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).
- Distinguish crawl prevention, exclusion with noindex and duplicate consolidation. A robots-blocked page cannot reliably expose its noindex/canonical; do not stack these as a universal fix. Pagination URLs should have their own canonical when they contain different products. [Robots directives](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag), [pagination](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/pagination-and-incremental-page-loading).
- A temporary stock gap ordinarily retains a useful page with truthful availability. For permanent removal, evaluate retained reference/support value first; genuinely gone content without an equivalent destination can return 404/410. There is no guaranteed 180-day deindexing clock. A 301 requires a verified relevant replacement, not a generic collection or homepage by default. [HTTP status effects](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/troubleshooting/http-status-codes), [site moves](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes).
- Google generative search uses normal search foundations. Do not promise exposure through special word counts, llms.txt, fabricated authority or FAQ templates. Other platforms need their own evidence and eligibility checks. [Google AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
