# Synthetic worked example

Input: Four synthetic order values 10,10,10,70 USD from a convenience sample. Mean=(10+10+10+70)/4=25; median 10; range 60. One large order contributes 70/100=70% of sampled revenue.

Result: the mean is higher than a typical sampled order; report both mean 25 and median 10. Do not remove 70 as an error without evidence, and do not estimate a population confidence interval from this fixture without a justified sampling model.

Boundary scenario 1: Ten rows come from the same customer. Do not treat them as ten independent customers when the estimand is customer behavior.
Boundary scenario 2: A comparison has p>0.05. Report the effect and uncertainty; do not write “the variants are equivalent” without an equivalence design.

Additional calculated fixture: in a simple random sample of 100 eligible orders,20 have a recorded care-question ticket. Sample proportion 20%; a95% Wilson interval with z=1.96 is approximately 13.34%–28.88%. This interval reflects binomial sampling assumptions and does not correct missing-ticket measurement or nonrandom sampling. It is not evidence that care instructions caused tickets.
