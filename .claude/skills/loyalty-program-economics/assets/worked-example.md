# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Synthetic rules earn 1 point per USD 1 of eligible net merchandise sales; 100 points redeem for USD 5. On USD 10,000 sales, 10,000 points create USD 500 face-value exposure. At an explicitly assumed 60% redemption, planning reward value is USD 300; it is not a proven accounting liability. Add USD 100 platform and USD 50 fulfillment/perk cost: USD 450 total planned cost. At USD 30 contribution per genuinely incremental order before program cost, 450/30 = 15 incremental orders are needed. A 100% redemption scenario raises cost to USD 650, requiring 21.67, or at least 22 whole incremental orders.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given no redemption history, show the full USD 500 exposure alongside scenarios; do not assert 40% breakage will occur.
2. Given members spend more than non-members without comparable assignment, do not treat the entire difference as incremental program revenue.
