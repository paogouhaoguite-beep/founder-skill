## Specific changes

A creator asset acceptance report with timestamped brief checks, claim/rights blockers and actionable revision feedback. This accepts a commissioned asset against its brief and contracted delivery; it does not release an assembled scheduled social post.

- Bind review to the exact asset version, brief and destination. A later edit requires rechecking affected claims, captions and disclosures.
- Compare the delivered work with contracted product, angle, format, required shots and CTA. Separate missing mandatory requirements from subjective style preferences.
- Check spoken, visual and text claims against evidence; a visual implication can overstate performance even when the caption is accurate.
- Review disclosure visibility, third-party assets and rights for the intended placement. Unknown permission is an unresolved item, not a numeric deduction that can be averaged away.
- Inspect hook/payoff alignment, legible captions, audible speech, product demonstration and destination continuity. Use actual playback or mark video-only checks unverified when only a transcript exists.
- Return accept, revise or unresolved with timestamped findings and actionable feedback. Approval applies only to that asset and use scope; publishing requires its own authorization.
