# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic descriptive readout: A has 1,000 eligible visitors and 40 purchases with USD 20 contribution/order: USD 800 or 0.80/visitor. B has 1,000 visitors and 35 purchases with USD 25 contribution: USD 875 or 0.875/visitor. B's conversion falls from 4% to 3.5%, while observed contribution/visitor rises 9.375%. This arithmetic alone provides neither an uncertainty interval nor a significant winner.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Reproduce 0.80, 0.875 and 9.375% while refusing to select a statistically proven winner without a valid analysis.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If B also received a new advertising campaign and a stockout, report confounding and propose an improved design; do not calculate causal elasticity.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
