# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Cleaner A costs $12/500 ml, dilution uses 10 ml per litre; B costs $15/750 ml and uses 15 ml per litre. A yields 50 litres and costs $0.24/litre; B yields 50 litres and costs $0.30/litre. Publish the calculation with dated prices and labelled dilution instructions. Do not conclude equal cleaning effectiveness from cost alone.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given one product’s dilution is absent, leave its cost-per-use unknown rather than copying a rival’s dilution.

2. Given millilitres and fluid ounces are mixed, normalize using a declared unit convention before comparison.
