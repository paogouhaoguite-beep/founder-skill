## Specific changes

A product offer review with accurate included value, contribution scenarios, conditions and ready-to-review copy.

- Write the current offer in plain language: what is included, what it costs, who qualifies, when it is available and what happens after purchase.
- Check the buyer’s actual desired outcome and practical friction from supplied evidence. Do not turn subjective value-equation scores into a forecast.
- Evaluate bundles, bonuses, guarantees and payment terms against contribution and fulfilment capacity. A bonus must have real availability and a defensible value description.
- Verify scarcity/deadlines, comparison prices and refund conditions. Distinguish a proposed commercial term from an existing customer entitlement.
- Produce alternative offer drafts with explicit tradeoffs and a controlled test plan; preserve full conditions near the decision, not hidden behind an attractive headline.
- Return a margin/claims checklist and the exact customer-facing offer copy. Do not change live prices, promises or policies without authorization.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
