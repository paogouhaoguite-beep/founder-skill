# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

For 100 synthetic units, quote A is USD 8/unit plus USD 200 freight and USD 100 confirmed other costs: USD 1,100 or 11/unit. B is USD 9/unit plus USD 100 freight with duties unknown: known subtotal USD 1,000 or 10/unit, but not a complete landed cost. Do not declare B cheaper until the missing cost and matched specifications are resolved.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Calculate A 11/unit and mark B 10/unit as a partial subtotal, preserving the unknown instead of treating it as zero.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If B is 100 packs of two while A is 100 single units, normalize actual units and MOQ commitment before a price ranking.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
