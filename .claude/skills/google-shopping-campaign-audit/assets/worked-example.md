# Synthetic worked example

All people, products, account results and amounts below are invented fixtures, not merchant outcomes. Amounts are USD unless noted.

Two synthetic items each generated USD 600 revenue and spent USD 200 (ROAS 3.0). Item A has USD 360 non-ad variable costs: pre-ad contribution 240, after-ad 40. Item B has USD 480 costs: pre-ad 120, after-ad −80. Their revenue break-even ROAS values, assuming stable mix and complete variable costs, are 600/240 = 2.5 and 600/120 = 5.0. A third approved item has zero impressions and is excluded from the campaign listing group; that exclusion is a delivery explanation, not proof it would sell if included.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given equal ROAS but the two different cost structures, distinguish A and B economically instead of applying one target to both.
2. Given missing Merchant Center access and zero impressions, report eligibility unknown and request diagnostics rather than label the product disapproved.
