# Public Creative Trend Scout

Use when a DTC seller needs current public creative inspiration for a niche, platform and market. Inputs: product facts, target shopper, geography/language, requested observation window, formats, exclusions and production capacity. Search publicly accessible sources when available; otherwise use supplied post links, screenshots or exports and call the result a supplied-sample review. Never fabricate inaccessible post content, private saves or audience data.

Build queries across niche problems, demonstrations, creators and format variants. For each retained item record canonical post/source, publication date, observation date, retrieval limits, format, opening device, payoff and observed metrics with their stated basis. A recent search result can describe an old post. Trace roundup claims to original posts where possible and avoid counting repeated articles as independent evidence.

Group by structural pattern rather than copied topic: comparison reveal, process demonstration, question-led carousel. Count unique posts and creators separately. Record actual recurrence within this sample; search ranking and survivor bias prevent declaring a platform-wide winner. Separate observed hook text from your proposed template, and quote only the necessary permitted excerpt.

Treat views, likes and comments as distinct metrics; unknown denominators preclude engagement rates. Post age, paid distribution and creator size can confound comparisons. Do not convert a publisher's adjective such as viral into a measured performance band. Build a benchmark only from a clearly defined comparable sample; otherwise return unknown.

For each potential content gap, link evidence of a shopper question and state the scope searched for existing answers. Absence from search results is not proof nobody serves the topic. Produce original, fact-checked merchant adaptations with a proposed payoff and realistic production needs. Choose a small test based on the merchant's capacity and predeclare outcomes/time window; do not promise the observed format will transfer performance.

Deliver an evidence table, format clusters, uncertainty notes and one concrete draft concept. No scraping behind access controls, media copying, creator outreach or publication is implied.
