# Synthetic worked example

Input: Brand “North Cup” sells bottles; logo must print in one color on a small cap and appear horizontally on a box.

Brief: calm, durable, legible; avoid mountain clichés already common in the supplied competitor set. Deliver wordmark plus compact mark, one-color versions and source vectors. Review at the actual cap imprint area supplied by the manufacturer; require a proof before production. Do not invent a minimum stroke thickness.

Boundary scenario 1: A concept uses an unlicensed font. Request license evidence or a suitable replacement before acceptance.
Boundary scenario 2: Merchant asks whether the mark is legally clear. Provide the visual evidence and route trademark clearance to the appropriate current review; do not certify it.
