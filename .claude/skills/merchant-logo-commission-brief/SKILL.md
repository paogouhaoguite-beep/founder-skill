# Merchant Logo Commission Brief

## Inputs
Collect brand name spelling, audience, product category, existing identity, desired character, competitors, mandatory symbols and actual applications such as labels, packaging, favicon or embroidery. A written brief works without design tools. Naming clearance is separate from logo design.

## Brief construction
Translate personality into paired constraints: for example calm rather than clinical, practical rather than industrial. Identify category conventions and where distinctiveness matters without copying competitor marks. Specify prohibited imagery and factual limitations.

Define application constraints before style: smallest intended size, one-color reproduction, dark/light backgrounds, square/long formats, print method and manufacturing tolerances. Ask the printer or manufacturer for real limits; do not impose generic line thickness or bleed values.

Request concepts with rationale and evaluation in actual use contexts. Review legibility, recognition, scaling, silhouette and consistency, separating subjective preference from a failed requirement. Do not declare trademark availability or promise originality from a visual comparison alone.

## Deliver
Return designer-ready brief, application mockup list, review rubric and deliverable checklist: vector master where appropriate, raster exports, color/mono versions, clear-space/use guidance, font rights and ownership/license agreement. Commissioning or accepting commercial terms requires authorization.
