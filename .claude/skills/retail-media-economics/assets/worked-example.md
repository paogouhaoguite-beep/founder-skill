# Worked example

All names, inputs and results below are synthetic.

Input: Retailer reports $10,000 consumer sales; merchant receives $6,000 wholesale revenue and incurs $3,500 product cost plus $1,000 media. Merchant contribution before other variable costs = $1,500. A proposed $300 co-op reimbursement is unapproved and excluded; if later approved, scenario contribution becomes $1,800. Do not call retailer ROAS 10× the merchant’s profit multiple.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Given store-visit attribution is modeled by the retailer, label it modeled and separate from verified orders.

2. Given the campaign only benefits retailer checkout, do not claim growth in the merchant’s owned customer list.
