# Retail Media Economics

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a retail-media commercial assessment separating retailer sales, merchant revenue, co-op funding and owned-store outcomes.

## Merchant inputs

Retail-media proposal; merchant direct-store and retail goals; SKU margins; funding/co-op terms; retailer reports; attribution window; stock and channel constraints.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Distinguish on-retailer conversion from traffic or purchases on the owned store. A DTC brand can advertise with a retailer, but the economic destination must remain explicit.

2. Separate media fees, retailer margin, co-op reimbursement, creative costs and promotional funding. Check eligibility and reimbursement evidence rather than netting an unapproved credit.

3. Map attributed retailer revenue to the merchant’s actual wholesale or retail economics; shelf sales at retail price are not automatically merchant revenue.

4. Review SKU availability and channel conflicts, and identify who owns product content, price and customer data.

5. Compare a no-campaign baseline or holdout when available; marketplace ranking and reported ROAS alone do not prove incrementality or a durable owned audience.

6. Return a commercial decision ledger with break-even assumptions, unknowns and evidence needed before accepting a retailer commitment.

## Deliverable

A retail-media commercial assessment separating retailer sales, merchant revenue, co-op funding and owned-store outcomes.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.
