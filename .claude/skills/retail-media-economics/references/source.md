## Specific changes

A retail-media commercial assessment separating retailer sales, merchant revenue, co-op funding and owned-store outcomes.

- Distinguish on-retailer conversion from traffic or purchases on the owned store. A DTC brand can advertise with a retailer, but the economic destination must remain explicit.
- Separate media fees, retailer margin, co-op reimbursement, creative costs and promotional funding. Check eligibility and reimbursement evidence rather than netting an unapproved credit.
- Map attributed retailer revenue to the merchant’s actual wholesale or retail economics; shelf sales at retail price are not automatically merchant revenue.
- Review SKU availability and channel conflicts, and identify who owns product content, price and customer data.
- Compare a no-campaign baseline or holdout when available; marketplace ranking and reported ROAS alone do not prove incrementality or a durable owned audience.
- Return a commercial decision ledger with break-even assumptions, unknowns and evidence needed before accepting a retailer commitment.

Removed upstream mandatory registries, benchmark gates, mutable connector paths and software-only assumptions. Replaced them with merchant-supplied artifacts and explicitly labeled decisions. Kept task-specific method; replaced fixed industry targets with declared merchant constraints. Synthetic cases below are authored for this adaptation, not claimed customer outcomes.
