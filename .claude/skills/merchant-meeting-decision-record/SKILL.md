# Merchant Meeting Decision Record

## Scope and evidence
Collect purpose, participants/roles, agenda, relevant source documents and dated notes or transcript. Calendar/Notion connectors are optional; a local Markdown document is sufficient. Do not create meetings, invite people or publish pages without authorization.

## Before and after
Before a meeting, separate decisions needed from information to share, identify missing context and write an agenda with owners and proposed time budgets. Context from private documents stays within the authorized audience. A generated agenda is not evidence that a meeting occurred.

Afterward, distinguish explicit decisions, proposals, unresolved questions and actions. Link each material entry to a timestamp or source note. Do not turn “we could” into “we agreed”, infer an owner from job title, or invent due dates. Mark unclear ownership or relative dates for confirmation using the meeting date/timezone.

Write action items as observable next steps with owner, due date if stated, dependency and completion evidence. Keep contradictory recollections visible until resolved. Maintain decisions with rationale and superseded status rather than silently rewriting history.

If a knowledge tool is authorized, search for the correct project/page before creating a new dated record and link related context. Preserve existing content and restrict updates to the requested scope; without access, provide the exact page-ready draft.

## Deliver
Return meeting brief or minutes, decision log, action table, open questions and source links. Remove unnecessary personal details. External sends, calendar changes and task assignments are separately scoped.
