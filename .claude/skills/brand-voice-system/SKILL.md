# Brand Voice System

Use this when a DTC merchant needs a source-backed brand voice guide with naming glossary, contextual tone matrix and before/after copy examples.

## Merchant inputs

Owned product and service copy with URLs/dates; approved brand position; audience; current product names; disallowed claims; intended channels and locales.

Separate supplied facts, observed evidence, assumptions and unavailable information. Request only missing inputs that change the decision; a partial evidence set supports a partial result, not invented measurements.

## Tools and fallback

Read supplied CSV, screenshots, page text or documents with available file tools. A browser or authorized read-only connector can verify current pages and provider documentation. No named commercial service, host registry or script is required. Without live access, use dated exports and label the access window; do not imply a live check. Verify current provider-specific formats and policies before implementing a platform change.

## Procedure

1. Collect the merchant’s own shipped material and separate website, customer-support, campaign and founder voice. Do not use competitors as evidence of this brand’s voice.

2. Extract diction, sentence rhythm, person, formality and tone changes from specific passages. Mark new-brand choices as proposed, not historically observed.

3. Build a naming table for products, variants and collections, including capitalization, discontinued synonyms and distinctions such as waterproof versus water-resistant.

4. Define stable voice traits with do/don’t examples, then a tone matrix for product education, delay apologies, promotions and community replies. A playful product voice need not be playful during a complaint.

5. Rewrite representative original passages without changing specifications, offer conditions or substantiated claims. Preserve factual uncertainty even if stronger wording sounds more persuasive.

6. Deliver dated rules and a change log. If an existing approved guide contradicts the inferred corpus, surface the conflict for the merchant instead of silently replacing it.

7. Validate the guide on a withheld owned sample: annotate observed agreement and counterexamples, refine context-specific rules and preserve exceptions. Source word/piece minimums are not proof of representativeness; disclose thin or uneven corpora instead of generating numeric certainty.

8. Audit surface vocabulary/punctuation, structural syntax/rhythm and semantic stance separately. Return deviation, source rule, reader consequence and a proposed rewrite while preserving facts and approved claims. Do not force a 200-word lexicon or VAS85 pass score; require observable examples and a context-specific review.

## Deliverable

A source-backed brand voice guide with naming glossary, contextual tone matrix and before/after copy examples.

Include input provenance and observation dates beside affected rows. Explain the decision and the uncertainty it depends on; preserve exclusions and unresolved rows so another operator can reproduce the result.

## Execution scope

Prepare the analysis and reviewable artifacts within the requested scope. Sending messages, publishing, changing audiences or budgets, signing terms and editing live store settings require authorization for that action. A proposed change is not a completed action; report completion only from provider or page readback. Do not copy credentials or customer contact details into reports.
