# Brand Podcast Recording Plan

## Inputs
Collect audience, episode purpose, guest expertise, approved topics, recording setup, distribution rights and length. Use a document plan when recording tools are absent. Recruitment and recording require explicit participant permissions; a content plan is not consent.

## Prepare and record
Choose an episode question that serves the audience. Build a story arc from concrete experiences, decisions and reflections rather than a list of promotional claims. Prepare neutral follow-ups and verify biographical/product facts. Do not script a guest testimonial as though it were spontaneous.

For remote recording, run a technical check: quiet environment, microphone position, headphones, local track format, storage and backup. Record separate local tracks when supported, plus a reference track/sync event. Confirm the participant knows what is being recorded and how it may be edited and shared.

During recording, note useful moments and retakes without interrupting the narrative unnecessarily. Afterward verify every file opens, both speakers exist, start/end synchronization and drift, and safe transfer within the agreed scope. A cloud upload indicator is not proof the complete recording arrived.

## Deliver
Return run of show, question/probe guide, technical checklist, consent status, asset manifest and edit notes. Separate episode recording from derivative clips and advertising rights; do not publish or send files automatically.
