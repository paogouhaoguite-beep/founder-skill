# Merchant Business Decision Brief

## Briefing inputs
Collect the decision maker, decision deadline, source evidence, options, constraints, costs, assumptions and preferred format. A one-page Markdown brief is sufficient; document tools are optional. Missing evidence remains a named decision risk, not filled with persuasive fiction.

## Build the argument
Lead with the decision and recommendation, then the evidence that changes it. Separate measured facts, estimates and assumptions. Compare a realistic baseline/no-change option with alternatives using consistent time horizons and cost scope. Include implementation effort, recurring cost, downside and reversibility.

For financial cases, distinguish revenue from contribution and cash from accounting profit. Show benefit timing and avoid counting saved staff hours as cash savings unless spending can actually change. Define ROI/payback/discount-rate assumptions only when those measures fit the decision; do not impose a universal rate.

Compress supporting material without stripping qualifiers. Use tables for option comparison and a short evidence appendix for source date and uncertainty. Every requested action names an owner, dependency and condition that would change the recommendation. Do not imply stakeholder agreement that was not recorded.

## Deliver
Return finished brief with recommendation, options, cost/benefit bridge, risks, implementation check and exact approval/decision needed. Creating the brief does not approve the investment or distribute it.
