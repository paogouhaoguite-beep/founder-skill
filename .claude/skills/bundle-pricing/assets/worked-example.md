# Synthetic worked example

All quantities, amounts, policies and company circumstances below are invented for arithmetic or decision review. They are not benchmarks.

Synthetic pack uses two units of A costing USD 4 each and one B costing 3; packing/shipping costs 5. Fixed variable cost C = 16. At price 25 and fee 4%, contribution = 25×0.96-16 = 8. For target 10, price = 26/0.96 = 27.0833; rounding upward to 27.09 gives at least 10 before fee-rounding differences. Available A 50 and B 18 allow min(25,18)=18 packs before other commitments.

## Acceptance case 1 — supported inputs

Return contribution 8 and stock limit 18, and distinguish rounding from actual checkout fee rules.

## Acceptance case 2 — boundary or missing evidence

If B stock is shared with standalone orders, show the allocation dependency instead of publishing availability for all 18 bundles automatically.

These cases specify expected behavior. Reading them or validating package syntax does not demonstrate live merchant acceptance.
