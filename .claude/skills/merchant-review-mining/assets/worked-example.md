# Synthetic reviews
R1: "Small fits my 13-inch laptop; not the 15-inch one." R2: "Arrived late but stitching looks good." R3 repeats R2 on a syndication site. Deduplicate R3. Corpus N=2: fit-limit theme 1/2, delivery-delay theme 1/2, positive construction perception 1/2. This does not establish a 50% shipping-delay rate among all orders.

## Acceptance scenarios
1. Reviews have ratings but no text. Report rating distribution and the absent qualitative evidence; do not invent reasons.
2. A minority reports a broken clasp while most praise design. Retain the defect signal and its source IDs; do not erase it because average sentiment is positive.
